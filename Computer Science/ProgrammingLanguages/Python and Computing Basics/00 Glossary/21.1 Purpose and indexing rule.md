# 21.1 Purpose and indexing rule

This master glossary consolidates terms defined at the ends of Chapters 1–20. Each entry reproduces the first controlling definition and identifies the section in which the term entered the cumulative vocabulary. A later chapter may refine a term by adding scope or implementation detail; such refinement supplements rather than silently replaces the earlier definition. When an ordinary word has a specialised computing, mathematical, statistical, or security meaning, the specialised meaning is stated here.

The glossary is an index, not a substitute for the derivation surrounding a definition. The cited section contains examples, misconceptions, and boundary cases. Alphabetisation ignores case. Terms beginning with symbols or underscores appear under “Symbols and special names.”
