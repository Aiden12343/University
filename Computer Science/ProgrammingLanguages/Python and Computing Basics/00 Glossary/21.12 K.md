# 21.12 K

- **Kernel:** the privileged core of an operating system. *(Introduced §2.17.)*
- **Key rotation:** controlled transition between versions of cryptographic or credential material, including adoption and retirement. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Keyword:** a word reserved by a language grammar. *(Introduced §5.22.)*
- **Keyword-only parameter:** a parameter that must receive an argument by keyword. *(Introduced §8.20.)*
- **Kill switch:** independently accessible mechanism to halt or disable an automated action at a defined safe boundary. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
