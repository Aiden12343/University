# 21.18 Q

- **Quality attribute:** required property such as security, performance, or reliability. *(Introduced §12.26.)*
- **Quantile:** value at or below which a specified proportion of a selected observation distribution lies under a stated convention. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Queue:** structure holding work awaiting service under ordering, capacity, durability, and admission semantics. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Queue age:** elapsed time since queued work entered or became eligible, often a stronger timeliness signal than depth alone. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Quota:** enforced allocation or ceiling on cumulative or concurrent resource use by a subject or scope. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
