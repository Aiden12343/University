# 21.22 U

- **Unallocated cost:** expenditure not yet attributable under the selected ownership and allocation model. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Undecidable problem:** a decision problem having no algorithm correct for every admitted instance. *(Introduced §3.16.)*
- **Unicode:** the standard assigning code points and properties across writing systems. *(Introduced §5.22.)*
- **Unit economics:** analysis of cost relative to a meaningful successful business or service outcome. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Unit test:** focused test of a small behavioural unit with controlled collaborators. *(Introduced §12.26.)*
- **Unknown value:** planned attribute whose result is unavailable until a later apply or remote operation. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Unsupervised learning:** learning structure without supplied target labels. *(Introduced §17.37.)*
- **Update strategy:** policy governing order, overlap, capacity, and health during workload replacement. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **URL:** structured resource locator containing components such as scheme, authority, path, and query. *(Introduced §18.36.)*
- **User journey:** sequence of interactions through which a user seeks one meaningful outcome, often spanning many service operations. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **User space:** the restricted execution domain used by ordinary applications. *(Introduced §2.17.)*
- **UTF-8:** a variable-width encoding of Unicode code points. *(Introduced §4.15.)*
- **Utilisation:** fraction or rate of a resource's selected usable capacity consumed over a measurement interval. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
