# 21.6 E

- **Editable installation:** an installation whose imports refer to a working source tree under a build tool’s mechanism. *(Introduced §4.15.)*
- **Effect size:** magnitude of a difference or association. *(Introduced §16.31.)*
- **Egress:** traffic leaving a workload, network segment, or other defined boundary. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Empirical risk:** average observed loss over a sample. *(Introduced §17.37.)*
- **Encapsulation:** ownership of state and invariants behind a controlled interface. *(Introduced §9.23.)*
- **Endpoint:** addressable network destination offered as a candidate for a logical service or operation. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Ensemble:** predictive system combining multiple fitted models. *(Introduced §17.37.)*
- **Entry point:** metadata identifying a callable or component to expose through an installed interface. *(Introduced §10.18.)*
- **Environment variable:** a named string supplied in a process environment. *(Introduced §3.16.)*
- **Ephemeral runner:** build or CI execution environment created for bounded work and discarded rather than reused persistently. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Epoch:** one conceptual traversal of a training dataset under a batching procedure. *(Introduced §17.37.)*
- **Equivalence partitioning:** division of inputs into classes expected to receive similar treatment. *(Introduced §12.26.)*
- **Error budget:** allowable bad-event fraction or amount implied by an SLO over its population and window. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Estimand:** population quantity or causal contrast an analysis aims to estimate. *(Introduced §16.31.)*
- **Estimator:** rule mapping samples to estimates. *(Introduced §16.31.)*
- **Evaluation loop:** conceptual CPython machinery dispatching and executing bytecode operations. *(Introduced §15.23.)*
- **Event time:** time at which an event occurred in its source domain, distinguished from collection or ingestion time. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Eventually consistent:** property under which replicas are expected to converge when specified update and communication conditions continue. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Exception chaining:** an explicit causal relationship between a newly raised exception and an earlier one. *(Introduced §6.20.)*
- **Executable file:** a structured persistent program representation accepted by a loader. *(Introduced §2.17.)*
- **Execution:** one concrete history of a running program. *(Introduced §3.16.)*
- **Execution plan:** machine-readable proposal of mutations derived from configuration, state, observations, and provider semantics. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
- **Exit status:** a process’s numeric completion classification supplied to its parent environment. *(Introduced §3.16.)*
- **Expected complexity:** average bound under a stated probability or distribution assumption. *(Introduced §7.18.)*
- **Expression:** syntax whose successful evaluation produces a value. *(Introduced §5.22.)*
- **External effect:** consequence occurring outside the local transaction or process, such as payment, email, deletion, or partner mutation. *(Introduced §20.38.)*
