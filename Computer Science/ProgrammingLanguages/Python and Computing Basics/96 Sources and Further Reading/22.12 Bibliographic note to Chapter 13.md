# 22.12 Bibliographic note to Chapter 13

The principal algorithm references are:

- Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, and Stein for broad algorithm design and proof;
- Knuth for exhaustive analysis and historical detail;
- Sedgewick and Wayne for implementation-centred exposition;
- Tarjan for foundational graph and disjoint-set analyses;
- Dijkstra for shortest-path and structured-programming contributions;
- Hoare for partitioning, axiomatic reasoning, and program correctness;
- Floyd for inductive assertions;
- Bellman for dynamic programming.

Big-O notation gives an asymptotic upper bound, not an exact runtime and not necessarily a tight bound. Chapter 13 uses \(\Theta\) for tight asymptotic growth where justified and states expected/amortised/worst-case qualifiers. Algorithmic correctness and implementation correctness are separate proof obligations connected by representation invariants.
