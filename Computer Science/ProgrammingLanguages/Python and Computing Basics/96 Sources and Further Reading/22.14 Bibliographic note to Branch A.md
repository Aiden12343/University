# 22.14 Bibliographic note to Branch A

Harris and colleagues describe NumPy’s role and architecture in their 2020 *Nature* article. NumPy’s official user guide and reference define array creation, dtypes, indexing, broadcasting, universal functions, and random generation. McKinney’s original pandas paper and *Python for Data Analysis* develop labelled tables and practical transformations; official pandas documentation governs current behaviour.

Statistical foundations are treated by Casella and Berger, Wasserman, and texts devoted to experimental design and causal inference. Efron and Tibshirani develop the bootstrap. Pearl, Hernán and Robins, and Imbens and Rubin give differing but rigorous causal frameworks. The American Statistical Association’s statement and later discussion clarify limitations of p-value thresholds.

Tukey, Cleveland, Tufte, and Wilkinson supply influential visualisation and graphical-grammar traditions. Visual choices must be judged by perceptual evidence, domain purpose, and accessibility rather than a single aesthetic authority.
