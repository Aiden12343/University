# 22.18 Bibliographic note to Branch E

Humble and Farley develop continuous delivery; Forsgren, Humble, and Kim report empirical research on delivery performance; Morris treats infrastructure as code. Kleppmann develops replication, partitioning, streams, and data-system trade-offs. Beyer and colleagues’ *Site Reliability Engineering* and companion workbook formalise SLOs, error budgets, monitoring, and incident practice.

The Open Container Initiative specifications define image, runtime, and distribution formats. Container runtime and orchestrator behaviour requires current official documentation. NIST SP 800-190 analyses container-security risks. Cloud-provider APIs and IAM semantics are provider-specific and versioned.

Reliability mathematics draws on queueing theory, probability, control, and distributed systems. Little’s Law has precise stability and averaging conditions. Percentiles, retries, and availability calculations must state their sample and dependence assumptions.
