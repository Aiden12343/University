# Glossary - Geometry & Trig Terms

Shape, angle, vector, and trigonometry words. All terms link back to their introducing topic and forward to CS applications.

## 4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles
- [[line]] - straight one-dimensional figure extending infinitely in both directions
- [[angle]] - the amount of turn between two rays sharing an endpoint
- [[parallel]] - two lines in the same plane that never meet
- [[triangle]] - a three-sided polygon with interior angles summing to 180°
- [[angle sum]] - total interior angles of a polygon (180° for triangles)

## 4.2 Perimeter Area and Volume
- [[perimeter]] - total distance around the edge of a shape
- [[area]] - amount of flat surface inside a boundary
- [[volume]] - amount of 3D space an object occupies
- [[unit squared]] - square unit (cm², m²) for area
- [[unit cubed]] - cubic unit (cm³, m³) for volume

## 4.3 Circle Geometry
- [[radius]] - distance from centre to edge of a circle
- [[diameter]] - distance across a circle through its centre
- [[circumference]] - total distance around a circle
- [[pi]] - ratio of circumference to diameter (≈ 3.14159)
- [[arc]] - a portion of the circumference

## 4.4 Pythagoras Theorem
- [[right angle]] - an angle of exactly 90°
- [[hypotenuse]] - longest side of a right triangle, opposite the right angle
- [[square of a side]] - a side length multiplied by itself
- [[distance formula]] - d = √((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²)
- [[checking triangle type]] - using a² + b² = c² to test for right angles

## 4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA
- [[sine]] - opposite/hypotenuse ratio
- [[cosine]] - adjacent/hypotenuse ratio
- [[tangent]] - opposite/adjacent ratio
- [[opposite]] - side across from a given angle
- [[adjacent]] - side next to a given angle (not the hypotenuse)

## 4.6 Unit Circle
- [[unit circle]] - circle of radius 1 centred at origin
- [[cosine coordinate]] - x-coordinate on the unit circle
- [[sine coordinate]] - y-coordinate on the unit circle
- [[period]] - interval after which a function repeats
- [[quadrant signs]] - which trig functions are positive in each quadrant

## 4.7 Radians and Degrees
- [[degree]] - unit where full circle = 360°
- [[radian]] - unit where full circle = 2π rad
- [[arc length]] - distance along circumference
- [[full turn]] - 360° or 2π radians
- [[conversion]] - π rad = 180°

## 4.8 Vectors Basics
- [[vector]] - quantity with magnitude and direction
- [[magnitude]] - length of a vector
- [[direction]] - orientation of a vector
- [[component]] - one coordinate value of a vector
- [[resultant]] - sum of two or more vectors

## 4.9 Dot Product and Projection
- [[dot product]] - a·b = a_x b_x + a_y b_y = |a||b|cos θ
- [[projection]] - component of one vector along another
- [[scalar]] - a single number (not a vector)
- [[orthogonal]] - perpendicular (dot product = 0)
- [[normal vector]] - vector perpendicular to a surface

## 4.10 Geometry for Computer Graphics
- [[model space]] - local coordinate system of a 3D object
- [[screen space]] - 2D pixel coordinates on a display
- [[rotation]] - turning an object around an axis
- [[scaling]] - enlarging or shrinking an object
- [[translation]] - moving an object by adding an offset
