# adjacent

In a right triangle, the side next to (adjacent to) a given angle, excluding the hypotenuse. The side that forms the angle along with the hypotenuse.

First introduced in: [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - identifying triangle sides for trigonometry
