# angle sum

The total of all interior angles in a polygon. For a [[triangle]] the angle sum is always 180°. For a quadrilateral it is 360°, and the pattern increases by 180° for each additional side (n-sided polygon sum = $(n-2) \times 180°$). The triangle angle sum of 180° acts as a consistency check — like a [[checksum]] in programming — if your three angles do not add to exactly 180°, at least one measurement is wrong.
