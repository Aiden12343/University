# angle

The amount of turn between two rays that share a common endpoint (vertex), measured in degrees (°). A full turn is 360°, a half-turn is 180°, and a quarter-turn (right angle) is 90°. Angles are a circular analogue of the linear [[number line]] — the number line wraps around from 0° to 360°.
