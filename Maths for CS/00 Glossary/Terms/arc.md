# arc

A portion of a circle's circumference between two points on the circle. An arc has a central angle measured at the centre.

First introduced in: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]], [[1.6 Fractions]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - rounded corners, curved paths, circular motion

## Related Notes
- [[circumference]] - the full circle that contains the arc
- [[radian]] - alternative unit for measuring arcs
- [[radius]] - needed to compute arc length
