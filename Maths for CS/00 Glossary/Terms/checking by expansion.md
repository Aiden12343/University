# checking by expansion

Verifying a factorisation by multiplying the bracketed factors back out to confirm the original expression is recovered.

First introduced in: [[2.4 Factorising]]
Builds on: [[2.3 Expanding Brackets]] and [[substitution]]
Used in CS: Test suites and symbolic algebra systems expand factored forms to verify that simplification and refactorisation steps preserved correctness.
