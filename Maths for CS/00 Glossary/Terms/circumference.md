# circumference

The total distance around the edge of a circle. The circle's perimeter.

First introduced in: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]]
Builds on: [[4.2 Perimeter Area and Volume]], [[1.4 Multiplication and Division]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - wheel rotations, circular motion

## Related Notes
- [[pi]] - circumference = π × diameter
- [[radius]] - circumference = 2πr
- [[arc]] - a portion of the circumference
