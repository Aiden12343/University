# common factor

A number or algebraic expression that divides exactly into every term of an expression.

First introduced in: [[2.4 Factorising]]
Builds on: [[factor]] from [[1.10 Factors Multiples and Primes]]
Used in CS: Compilers extract common subexpressions by recognising repeated factors in code, similar to taking out a common factor algebraically.
