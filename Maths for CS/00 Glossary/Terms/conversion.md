# conversion

The process of changing between degrees and radians using the factor π rad = 180°. Radians = degrees × π/180. Degrees = radians × 180/π.

First introduced in: [[4.7 Radians and Degrees]]
Builds on: [[1.6 Fractions]], [[1.4 Multiplication and Division]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - converting user-facing degrees to code-ready radians
