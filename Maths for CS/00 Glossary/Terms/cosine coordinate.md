# cosine coordinate

The x-coordinate of a point on the unit circle at angle θ, equal to cos θ. Represents the horizontal component of a direction vector.

First introduced in: [[4.6 Unit Circle]]
Builds on: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]], [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - horizontal direction, rotation x-component
