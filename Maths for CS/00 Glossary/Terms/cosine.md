# cosine

In a right triangle, the ratio of the adjacent side to the hypotenuse. On the unit circle, the x-coordinate at a given angle.

First introduced in: [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
Builds on: [[4.4 Pythagoras Theorem]], [[1.6 Fractions]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - rotation matrices, lighting, audio
