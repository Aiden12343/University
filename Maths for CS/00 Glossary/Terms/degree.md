# degree

A unit of angle measurement where a full circle equals 360°. One degree is 1/360 of a full turn. Symbol: °.

First introduced in: [[4.7 Radians and Degrees]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]], [[1.6 Fractions]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - user-facing angle display, Unity editor rotation
