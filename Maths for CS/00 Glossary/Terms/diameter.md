# diameter

The distance across a circle passing through its centre. Twice the radius.

First introduced in: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]], [[1.4 Multiplication and Division]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - circle dimensions in graphics

## Related Notes
- [[radius]] - half the diameter
- [[circumference]] - π × diameter
- [[pi]] - the ratio of circumference to diameter
