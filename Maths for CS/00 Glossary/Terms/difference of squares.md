# difference of squares

The special pattern a² - b² that factorises to (a + b)(a - b), where two perfect squares are subtracted.

First introduced in: [[2.4 Factorising]]
Builds on: [[2.3 Expanding Brackets]] and [[1.4 Multiplication and Times Tables]]
Used in CS: Fast numeric algorithms and symbolic simplifiers recognise the difference-of-squares pattern to factorise expressions in one step.
