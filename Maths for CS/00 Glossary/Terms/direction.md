# direction

The way a vector points. Given as an angle from a reference axis or as a unit vector. Direction is independent of magnitude.

First introduced in: [[4.8 Vectors Basics]]
Builds on: [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]], [[4.7 Radians and Degrees]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - character facing direction, camera direction

## Related Notes

- [[unit vector]] - isolates direction from magnitude
- [[magnitude]] - the size companion to direction
