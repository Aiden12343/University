# distributive law

The rule that a(b + c) = ab + ac, meaning the outside multiplier applies to every term inside the brackets.

First introduced in: [[2.3 Expanding Brackets]]
Builds on: [[1.4 Multiplication and Times Tables]]
Used in CS: Compilers and symbolic tools apply the distributive law when expanding compact expressions into explicit multiplications.
