# factor pair

Two numbers or expressions that multiply together to give a particular product, such as 2 and 3 for the constant term in x² + 5x + 6.

First introduced in: [[2.4 Factorising]]
Builds on: [[factor]] from [[1.10 Factors Multiples and Primes]]
Used in CS: Quadratic and polynomial algorithms in graphics and cryptography search for factor pairs to decompose expressions efficiently.
