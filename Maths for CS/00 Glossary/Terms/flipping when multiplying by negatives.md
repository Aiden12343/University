# flipping when multiplying by negatives

The rule that the inequality sign must be reversed when both sides are multiplied or divided by a negative number.

First introduced in: [[2.7 Inequalities]]
Builds on: [[1.8 Negative Numbers]], [[balancing]]
Used in CS: Inverting comparisons after negating a value (e.g. when transforming `-cost > -threshold` to `cost < threshold`) requires flipping the sign to keep the logic equivalent.
