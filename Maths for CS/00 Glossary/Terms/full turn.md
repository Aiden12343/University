# full turn

A complete rotation of 360° or 2π radians. One full rotation returns a point on the unit circle to its starting position.

First introduced in: [[4.7 Radians and Degrees]]
Builds on: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]], [[4.6 Unit Circle]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - animation cycles, angular measurement

## Related Notes

- [[radian]] - 2π rad in a full turn
