# gradient
The steepness of a line, found as change in y divided by change in x.
First introduced in: [[3.3 Linear Functions and Gradient]]
Builds on: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]], [[1.6 Fractions]]
Used in CS: Slopes in graphics, learning rates, and trend lines all use gradient as rate of change.
