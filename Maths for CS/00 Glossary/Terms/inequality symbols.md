# inequality symbols

The comparison signs `<`, `>`, `≤`, and `≥` used to show that one quantity is less than or greater than another, with or without equality.

First introduced in: [[2.7 Inequalities]]
Builds on: [[less than and greater than]], [[1.1 Counting and Number Sense]]
Used in CS: Branch conditions (`if x > 0`), validation rules, and complexity bounds all rely on inequality symbols.
