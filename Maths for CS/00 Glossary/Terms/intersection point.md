# intersection point

The ordered pair of values where two equations are both true at once; on a graph, where their lines cross.

First introduced in: [[2.8 Simultaneous Equations]]

Builds on: [[2.5 Solving Linear Equations]]

Used in CS: Finding where two paths, rays, budget lines, or layout constraints meet in graphics and simulation.
