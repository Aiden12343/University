# like terms

Terms that have exactly the same variable part, so their coefficients can be added or subtracted.

First introduced in: [[2.2 Simplifying Expressions]]
Builds on: [[term]] and [[coefficient]]
Used in CS: Algebraic simplifiers merge like-term nodes in expression trees to reduce redundant computation.
