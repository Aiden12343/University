# normal vector

A vector perpendicular to a surface. In 3D graphics, every triangle has a surface normal used for lighting calculations.

First introduced in: [[4.9 Dot Product and Projection]]
Builds on: [[4.8 Vectors Basics]], [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - Phong lighting, back-face culling, [[11.14 Ray Tracing and Rendering Maths]]

## Related Notes
- [[orthogonal]] - a normal is orthogonal to every vector in the surface
- [[dot product]] - N·L gives diffuse brightness
- [[projection]] - projecting light onto the normal
