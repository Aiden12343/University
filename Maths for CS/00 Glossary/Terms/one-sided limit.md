# one-sided limit

The limit taken from only the left or only the right side of a point on the number line.

First introduced in: [[9.1 Limits and Continuity]]

Builds on: [[1.2 Place Value and Number Lines]]

Used in CS: Limits make rates of change and numerical approximations precise.
