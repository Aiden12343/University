# origin
The point (0, 0) where the x-axis and y-axis cross on a coordinate plane.
First introduced in: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]]
Builds on: [[zero as a number]], [[1.2 Place Value and Number Lines]]
Used in CS: Graphics systems choose an origin at the centre or top-left of a canvas; all positions are measured relative to it.
