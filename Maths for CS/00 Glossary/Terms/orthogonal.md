# orthogonal

Two vectors are orthogonal if their dot product is zero, meaning they are perpendicular (at 90°). Also called perpendicular in 2D/3D.

First introduced in: [[4.9 Dot Product and Projection]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]], [[4.8 Vectors Basics]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - coordinate axes, normal vectors, separation of concerns
