# parallel

Two lines in the same plane that never meet, no matter how far extended in either direction. Denoted with the symbol $\parallel$ (e.g., $l \parallel m$ means line $l$ is parallel to line $m$). Parallel lines maintain a constant distance between them at every point. When a [[transversal]] crosses parallel lines, corresponding angles are equal, alternate angles are equal, and co-interior angles sum to 180°.
