# pi

The constant ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, approximately 3.1415926535... An irrational number with infinite non-repeating digits.

First introduced in: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]]
Builds on: [[1.4 Multiplication and Division]], [[4.2 Perimeter Area and Volume]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - all circle/rotation calculations, [[6.3 Modular Arithmetic]] - periodic functions
