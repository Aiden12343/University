# projection

The component of one vector along the direction of another. proj_a(b) = (a·b / |a|²) a. Answers: "how much of b points in the direction of a?"

First introduced in: [[4.9 Dot Product and Projection]]
Builds on: [[4.8 Vectors Basics]], [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
