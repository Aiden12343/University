# quadrant signs

The pattern of which trigonometric functions are positive in each of the four quadrants of the coordinate plane. Remembered by CAST: Cosine (IV), All (I), Sine (II), Tangent (III).

First introduced in: [[4.6 Unit Circle]]
Builds on: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]], [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - angle determination from coordinates
