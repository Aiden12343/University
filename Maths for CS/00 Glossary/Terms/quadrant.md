# quadrant
One of the four regions of the coordinate plane, numbered I to IV, formed by the axes.
First introduced in: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]]
Builds on: [[x-axis]], [[y-axis]], [[1.8 Negative Numbers]]
Used in CS: Sign checks on x and y quickly locate which screen region or graph area a point belongs to.
