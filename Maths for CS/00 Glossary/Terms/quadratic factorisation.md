# quadratic factorisation

Writing a quadratic expression, such as x² + 5x + 6, as the product of two linear bracketed factors.

First introduced in: [[2.4 Factorising]]
Builds on: [[factor pair]] and [[2.3 Expanding Brackets]]
Used in CS: Symbolic algebra tools like SymPy factorise quadratics to simplify polynomials used in graphics, physics simulations, and optimisation.
