# radius

The distance from the centre of a circle to any point on its edge. Half the diameter.

First introduced in: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - circular hitboxes, drawing circles

## Related Notes
- [[diameter]] - twice the radius
- [[circumference]] - depends on radius
- [[pi]] - used with radius to compute circumference and area
