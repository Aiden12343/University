# resultant

The single vector that has the same effect as two or more vectors combined. Found by adding vectors tip-to-tail.

First introduced in: [[4.8 Vectors Basics]]
Builds on: [[1.3 Addition and Subtraction]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - combining forces, velocities, or movements

## Related Notes
- [[vector]] - the resultant is also a vector
- [[component]] - adding components gives the resultant
- [[magnitude]] - |a+b| is not simply |a|+|b|
