# right angle

An angle of exactly 90°, marked with a small square in geometric diagrams. One quarter of a full turn.

First introduced in: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]]
Builds on: [[1.3 Addition and Subtraction]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - perpendicular directions, coordinate axes
