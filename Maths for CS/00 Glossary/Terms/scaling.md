# scaling

Enlarging or shrinking an object by multiplying its coordinates by a scale factor. Not a rigid transformation (changes distance between points).

First introduced in: [[4.10 Geometry for Computer Graphics]]
Builds on: [[1.4 Multiplication and Division]], [[4.8 Vectors Basics]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - resizing objects, zoom, level-of-detail

## Related Notes
- [[translation]] - moves without scaling
- [[rotation]] - rotates without scaling
- [[model space]] - scaling may happen in model or world space
