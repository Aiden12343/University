# sine coordinate

The y-coordinate of a point on the unit circle at angle θ, equal to sin θ. Represents the vertical component of a direction vector.

First introduced in: [[4.6 Unit Circle]]
Builds on: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]], [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - vertical direction, rotation y-component
