# square of a side

The value obtained by multiplying a side length by itself. If a side has length s, its square is s² = s × s.

First introduced in: [[4.4 Pythagoras Theorem]]
Builds on: [[1.4 Multiplication and Division]], [[2.9 Indices and Powers]]
Used in CS: [[7.3 Norms and Distance]] - squared Euclidean distance avoids square roots
