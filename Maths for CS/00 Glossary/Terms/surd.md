# surd

An irrational root left in exact form, such as √2 or 3√5, rather than rounded to a decimal.

First introduced in: [[2.10 Surds and Roots]]

Builds on: [[square root]], [[1.10 Factors Multiples and Primes]]

Used in CS: Keeps geometry and algebra exact before floating-point approximation introduces error.
