# tangent

In a right triangle, the ratio of the opposite side to the adjacent side. Equal to sine divided by cosine. Also a line that touches a circle at exactly one point.

First introduced in: [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]
Builds on: [[4.1 Lines Angles and Triangles]], [[1.6 Fractions]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - slope calculations, aiming angles
