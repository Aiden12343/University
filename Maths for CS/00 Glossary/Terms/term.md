# term

One part of an expression, separated from other parts by plus or minus signs.

First introduced in: [[2.2 Simplifying Expressions]]
Builds on: [[expression]] from [[2.1 Variables Expressions and Equations]]
Used in CS: Compilers split expressions into term-level nodes when building abstract syntax trees.
