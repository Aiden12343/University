# triangle

A three-sided polygon whose interior [[angle sum]] is always 180°. Three non-collinear points always define a triangle. Triangles are classified by side lengths (scalene, isosceles, equilateral) and by angles (acute, right, obtuse). In computer graphics, triangles are the fundamental rendering primitive because three points are guaranteed to be coplanar.
