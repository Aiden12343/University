# two-step equation

A linear equation that requires two inverse operations, in reverse order of operations, to isolate the unknown.

First introduced in: [[2.5 Solving Linear Equations]]
Builds on: [[one-step equation]], [[1.9 Order of Operations]]
Used in CS: Formulas with both scaling and offset (e.g. `2 * width + margin = total`) map to two-step equations when solving for one variable.
