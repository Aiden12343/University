# unit circle

A circle of radius 1 centred at the origin (0, 0). Points on the unit circle have coordinates (cos θ, sin θ), defining sine and cosine for all angles.

First introduced in: [[4.6 Unit Circle]]
Builds on: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]], [[4.4 Pythagoras Theorem]], [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]]
Used in CS: [[10.4 Computer Graphics and Transformations]] - rotation, oscillations, signal generation
