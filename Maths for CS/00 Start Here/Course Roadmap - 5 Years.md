# Course Roadmap - 5 Years

## Year 0 - Foundation
Start with [[01 MOC - Number Foundations]], then [[02 MOC - Algebra]] and early graph work in [[03 MOC - Functions and Graphs]].

## Year 1 - Core CS Maths
Focus on [[05 MOC - Sets Logic and Proof]], [[06 MOC - Discrete Maths]], first probability, and introductory matrices.

## Year 2 - Algorithms Data and Calculus
Deepen [[07 MOC - Linear Algebra]], [[08 MOC - Probability and Statistics]], [[09 MOC - Calculus]], and algorithmic applications.

## Year 3 - Advanced Undergraduate
Study automata, computability, complexity, numerical methods, graph algorithms, cryptography, and statistical learning.

## Years 4-5 - Masters Preparation
Use [[11 MOC - Advanced Topics]] for advanced algorithms, ML maths, optimisation, type theory, quantum, post-quantum security, and research-paper maths.

## Rule
Progress is controlled by mastery tests, not by time spent reading.
