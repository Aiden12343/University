# 09 MOC - Calculus

Study change, accumulation, optimisation, and multivariable gradients.

## Topics
1. [[9.1 Limits and Continuity]] — foundation of all calculus; describes what values approach and when graphs have no breaks
2. [[9.2 Differentiation Basics]] — instantaneous rate of change; f'(x) = lim_{h→0}(f(x+h)-f(x))/h
3. [[9.3 Differentiation Rules]] — product, quotient, chain, and L'Hôpital's rule
4. [[9.4 Applications of Differentiation]] — critical points, optimisation, curve sketching, linear approximation
5. [[9.5 Integration Basics]] — antiderivatives, definite integrals, FTC, area under curve
6. [[9.6 Integration Techniques]] — u-substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions, numerical integration
7. [[9.7 Differential Equations Intro]] — separable ODEs, exponential growth/decay, logistic growth
8. [[9.8 Multivariable Functions]] — partial derivatives, gradient, Hessian, saddle points
9. [[9.9 Gradient Descent and Optimisation]] — GD update rule, learning rate, SGD vs batch, momentum, Adam
10. [[9.10 Convex Optimisation Basics]] — convex functions and sets, global minima, Lagrangian duality
