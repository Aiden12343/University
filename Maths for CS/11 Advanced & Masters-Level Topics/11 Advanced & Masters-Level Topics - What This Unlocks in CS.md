# 11 Advanced & Masters-Level Topics - What This Unlocks in CS

Prepare for master's-level AI, algorithms, security, theory, and research-heavy CS.

## CS Connections
- [[11.1 Advanced Complexity Theory]] - Complexity theory explains what efficient computation may or may not achieve.
- [[11.2 Randomised Algorithms]] - Randomisation helps hashing, primality tests, sampling, and distributed systems.
- [[11.3 Approximation Algorithms]] - Approximation is essential for scheduling, routing, and optimisation problems.
- [[11.4 Advanced Graph Theory]] - Advanced graph theory supports networks, compilers, logistics, and verification.
- [[11.5 Statistical Learning Theory]] - Learning theory explains when ML models may work beyond training data.
- [[11.6 Probabilistic Graphical Models]] - PGMs support diagnosis, NLP, robotics, and causal modelling.
- [[11.7 Advanced Optimisation for ML]] - Training deep learning systems is largely an optimisation problem.
- [[11.8 Multivariable Calculus for Deep Learning]] - Backpropagation is multivariable calculus implemented at scale.
- [[11.9 Linear Algebra for Representation Learning]] - Representation learning turns data into useful vector spaces.
- [[11.10 Numerical Methods and Stability]] - Scientific computing and ML depend on stable numerical approximations.
- [[11.11 Category Theory for CS Intro]] - Functional programming and semantics borrow ideas from category theory.
- [[11.12 Lambda Calculus and Formal Semantics]] - Programming language theory uses lambda calculus as a core model.
- [[11.13 Finite Fields and Polynomial Arithmetic]] - Finite fields power error correction and cryptographic protocols.
- [[11.14 Elliptic Curve Cryptography]] - ECC secures many modern systems with smaller keys than RSA.
- [[11.15 Lattice-Based Cryptography]] - Lattice cryptography is a leading path for post-quantum systems.
- [[11.16 Quantum Computing Maths Intro]] - Quantum computing changes assumptions in algorithms and cryptography.
- [[11.17 Information Theory for ML and Compression]] - Modern ML losses and compression theory share information-theoretic roots.
- [[11.18 Research Methods and Mathematical Writing]] - Master's projects require precise technical communication.
