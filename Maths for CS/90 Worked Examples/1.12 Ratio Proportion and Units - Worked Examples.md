# 1.12 Ratio Proportion and Units - Worked Examples

Theory note: [[1.12 Ratio Proportion and Units]]

> [!tip] Worked example routine

## Example 1 - Simplifying a Ratio
Question: Simplify `24:36`.

### Step 1 - Find the HCF

```text
HCF(24, 36) = 12
```

### Step 2 - Divide both parts by 12

```text
24 / 12 = 2
36 / 12 = 3
```

Answer:

```text
24:36 = 2:3
```

## Example 2 - Scaling a Ratio
Question: Scale `4:7` by 5.

### Step 1 - Multiply every part by 5

```text
4 x 5 = 20
7 x 5 = 35
```

Answer:

```text
4:7 = 20:35
```

## Example 3 - Sharing in a Ratio
Question: Share 72 in ratio `5:7`.

### Step 1 - Add ratio parts

```text
5 + 7 = 12 parts
```

### Step 2 - Find one part

```text
72 / 12 = 6
```

### Step 3 - Scale each part

```text
5 x 6 = 30
7 x 6 = 42
```

Answer: The shares are **30 and 42**.

## Example 4 - Unit Rate
Question: A server handles 900 requests in 30 seconds. How many requests per second is that?

### Step 1 - Divide requests by seconds

```text
900 / 30 = 30
```

Answer:

```text
30 requests/second
```

## Example 5 - Direct Proportion
Question: 4 sensors cost 28 pounds. How much do 9 sensors cost at the same rate?

### Step 1 - Find cost per sensor

```text
28 / 4 = 7 pounds per sensor
```

### Step 2 - Multiply by 9

```text
9 x 7 = 63
```

Answer: 9 sensors cost **63 pounds**.

## Example 6 - Unit Conversion
Question: Convert 7 seconds to milliseconds.

### Step 1 - Use the conversion

```text
1 second = 1000 milliseconds
```

### Step 2 - Multiply

```text
7 x 1000 = 7000
```

Answer: 7 seconds = **7000 ms**.

## Example 7 - Screen Ratio
Question: Simplify `2560:1440`.

### Step 1 - Divide both by 10 first

```text
256:144
```

### Step 2 - Divide both by 16

```text
256 / 16 = 16
144 / 16 = 9
```

Answer:

```text
2560:1440 = 16:9
```

## Example 8 - Benchmark Comparison
Question: Program A processes 600 records in 10 seconds. Program B processes 420 records in 6 seconds. Which has higher throughput?

### Step 1 - Find A's unit rate

```text
600 / 10 = 60 records/second
```

### Step 2 - Find B's unit rate

```text
420 / 6 = 70 records/second
```

Answer: Program B has higher throughput.
