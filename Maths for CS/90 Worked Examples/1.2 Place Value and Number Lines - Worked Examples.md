## Example 1 - Reading Place Value
Question: What is the value of the digit `8` in `582`?

### Step 1 - Write the place-value table

```text
Hundreds | Tens | Ones
   5     |  8   |  2
```

### Step 2 - Locate the digit
The digit `8` is in the tens column.

### Step 3 - Convert the place to value
8 tens means:

```text
8 x 10 = 80
```

Answer: The value of `8` is **80**.

## Example 2 - Same Digit, Different Value
Question: Compare the value of `3` in `36` and `306`.

### Step 1 - Read `36`

```text
Tens | Ones
 3   |  6
```

In `36`, the `3` means 3 tens.

```text
3 tens = 30
```

### Step 2 - Read `306`

```text
Hundreds | Tens | Ones
   3     |  0   |  6
```

In `306`, the `3` means 3 hundreds.

```text
3 hundreds = 300
```

Answer: The same digit has value 30 in `36` and value 300 in `306`.

## Example 3 - Expanded Form
Question: Write `4,209` in expanded form.

### Step 1 - Place the digits

```text
Thousands | Hundreds | Tens | Ones
    4     |    2     |  0   |  9
```

### Step 2 - Convert each digit to value

```text
4 thousands = 4000
2 hundreds = 200
0 tens = 0
9 ones = 9
```

### Step 3 - Write the sum

```text
4,209 = 4000 + 200 + 0 + 9
```

### Step 4 - Remove the zero part if you want a cleaner answer

```text
4,209 = 4000 + 200 + 9
```

Answer: `4,209 = 4000 + 200 + 9`.

## Example 4 - Comparing Large Numbers
Question: Which is greater, `7,305` or `7,350`?

### Step 1 - Compare thousands

```text
7,305
7,350
^ same
```

### Step 2 - Compare hundreds

```text
7,305
7,350
  ^ same
```

### Step 3 - Compare tens

```text
7,305
7,350
   ^ different
```

The first number has 0 tens. The second number has 5 tens.

### Step 4 - Decide
5 tens is greater than 0 tens.

Answer:

```text
7,350 > 7,305
```

## Example 5 - Placing a Number on a Number Line
Question: Place `64` on a number line marked by tens.

### Step 1 - Draw tens landmarks

```text
0 --- 10 --- 20 --- 30 --- 40 --- 50 --- 60 --- 70
```

### Step 2 - Find the two landmarks around 64

```text
60 < 64 < 70
```

### Step 3 - Decide where it sits
64 is 4 steps after 60 and 6 steps before 70, so it is a little less than halfway from 60 to 70.

```text
60 --- 61 --- 62 --- 63 --- 64 --- 65 --- 66 --- 67 --- 68 --- 69 --- 70
                         ^
```

Answer: 64 sits between 60 and 70, closer to 60.

## Example 6 - Rounding to the Nearest 10
Question: Round `83` to the nearest 10.

### Step 1 - Find the two tens landmarks

```text
80 < 83 < 90
```

### Step 2 - Compare distances

```text
83 is 3 away from 80
83 is 7 away from 90
```

### Step 3 - Choose the closer landmark
83 is closer to 80.

Answer: `83` rounds to **80**.

## Example 8 - Computer Science Position Meaning
Question: Why can `10` mean ten in decimal but two in binary?

### Step 1 - Read decimal place values

```text
Decimal 10:
Tens | Ones
 1   |  0
```

So decimal `10` means:

```text
1 ten + 0 ones = 10
```

### Step 2 - Read binary place values

```text
Binary 10:
Twos | Ones
 1   |  0
```

So binary `10` means:

```text
1 two + 0 ones = 2
```

Answer: The written digits are the same, but the place values are different because the base is different.
