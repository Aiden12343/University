# 1.5 Division and Remainders - Worked Examples

Theory note: [[1.5 Division and Remainders]]

Use these before attempting [[1.5 Division and Remainders - Practice Questions]].

> [!tip] Worked example routine
> After every division, check with multiplication. If there is a remainder, multiply first and then add the remainder.

## Example 1 - Sharing Equally
Question: Share 20 counters equally between 4 people. How many counters does each person get?

### Step 1 - Identify the total

```text
20 counters
```

### Step 2 - Identify how many people share them

```text
4 people
```

### Step 3 - Divide

```text
20 / 4 = 5
```

Answer: Each person gets **5 counters**.

### Step 4 - Check

```text
4 x 5 = 20
```

The check works.

## Example 2 - Grouping
Question: You have 32 batteries. Each device needs 4 batteries. How many devices can you fill?

### Step 1 - Identify the total

```text
32 batteries
```

### Step 2 - Identify the group size

```text
4 batteries per device
```

### Step 3 - Divide

```text
32 / 4 = 8
```

Answer: You can fill **8 devices**.

## Example 3 - Remainder
Question: Divide 29 by 6.

### Step 1 - Find nearby multiplication facts

```text
6 x 4 = 24
6 x 5 = 30
```

30 is too high, so use 24.

### Step 2 - Write the quotient

```text
29 / 6 = 4 remainder something
```

### Step 3 - Find the remainder

```text
29 - 24 = 5
```

Answer:

```text
29 / 6 = 4 r5
```

### Step 4 - Check

```text
6 x 4 + 5 = 29
```

The check works.

## Example 4 - Remainder Must Be Smaller
Question: Is `31 / 7 = 3 r10` correct?

### Step 1 - Check the remainder
The divisor is 7. The remainder is 10.

The remainder is too large because another group of 7 can be made.

### Step 2 - Use a better multiplication fact

```text
7 x 4 = 28
```

### Step 3 - Find the leftover

```text
31 - 28 = 3
```

Answer:

```text
31 / 7 = 4 r3
```

## Example 5 - Division by 10
Question: Calculate `560 / 10`.

### Step 1 - Use place value
Dividing by 10 moves digits one place right.

```text
560 -> 56
```

Answer:

```text
560 / 10 = 56
```

## Example 6 - Division by 100
Question: Calculate `4,300 / 100`.

### Step 1 - Use place value
Dividing by 100 moves digits two places right.

```text
4300 -> 43
```

Answer:

```text
4300 / 100 = 43
```

## Example 7 - Pagination
Question: A search page displays 12 results per page. There are 50 results. How many full pages are there, and how many results remain?

### Step 1 - Translate to division

```text
50 / 12
```

### Step 2 - Find the largest useful multiple of 12

```text
12 x 4 = 48
12 x 5 = 60
```

60 is too high.

### Step 3 - Find the remainder

```text
50 - 48 = 2
```

Answer:

```text
50 / 12 = 4 r2
```

There are 4 full pages and 2 results left for another page.

In practice, the site needs 5 pages total.

## Example 8 - Even or Odd
Question: How can division by 2 tell whether 17 is even or odd?

### Step 1 - Divide by 2

```text
17 / 2 = 8 r1
```

### Step 2 - Interpret the remainder
There is a remainder of 1, so 17 is not exactly divisible by 2.

Answer: 17 is **odd**.

## Self-Check
You understand these examples when you can:

- find a quotient using multiplication facts
- find and check a remainder
- reject a remainder that is too large
- divide by 10 and 100 using place value
- explain pagination using quotient and remainder
