# 1.7 Decimals and Percentages - Worked Examples

Theory note: [[1.7 Decimals and Percentages]]

Use these before attempting [[1.7 Decimals and Percentages - Practice Questions]].

> [!tip] Worked example routine
> Say whether the number is a fraction, decimal, or percentage. Then convert it into one of the other forms to check meaning.

## Example 1 - Reading a Decimal
Question: What does `0.48` mean?

### Step 1 - Read decimal places
The 4 is in the tenths place.

The 8 is in the hundredths place.

### Step 2 - Convert to hundredths

```text
0.48 = 48/100
```

Answer: `0.48` means **48 hundredths**.

## Example 2 - Comparing Decimals
Question: Which is larger, `0.8` or `0.75`?

### Step 1 - Write both to hundredths

```text
0.8 = 0.80
0.75 = 0.75
```

### Step 2 - Compare

```text
80 hundredths > 75 hundredths
```

Answer:

```text
0.8 > 0.75
```

## Example 3 - Fraction to Decimal
Question: Convert `7/10` to a decimal.

### Step 1 - Read the denominator
Tenths become the first digit after the decimal point.

### Step 2 - Convert

```text
7/10 = 0.7
```

Answer: `0.7`.

## Example 4 - Fraction to Percentage
Question: Convert `3/4` to a percentage.

### Step 1 - Convert to hundredths

```text
3/4 = 75/100
```

### Step 2 - Percent means out of 100

```text
75/100 = 75%
```

Answer: `3/4 = 75%`.

## Example 5 - Percentage to Decimal
Question: Convert `62%` to a decimal.

### Step 1 - Percent means per hundred

```text
62% = 62/100
```

### Step 2 - Write as a decimal

```text
62/100 = 0.62
```

Answer: `62% = 0.62`.

## Example 6 - Percentage of an Amount
Question: Find 20% of 150.

### Step 1 - Convert 20%

```text
20% = 1/5
```

### Step 2 - Find one fifth

```text
150 / 5 = 30
```

Answer: 20% of 150 is **30**.

## Example 7 - Percentage Increase
Question: Increase 80 by 25%.

### Step 1 - Find 25% of 80

```text
25% = 1/4
80 / 4 = 20
```

### Step 2 - Add the increase

```text
80 + 20 = 100
```

Answer: 80 increased by 25% is **100**.

## Example 8 - Percentage Decrease
Question: Decrease 60 by 10%.

### Step 1 - Find 10% of 60

```text
10% = 1/10
60 / 10 = 6
```

### Step 2 - Subtract the decrease

```text
60 - 6 = 54
```

Answer: 60 decreased by 10% is **54**.

## Example 9 - Memory Usage
Question: A computer uses 12 GB out of 16 GB memory. What percentage is used?

### Step 1 - Write the fraction

```text
12/16
```

### Step 2 - Simplify

```text
12/16 = 3/4
```

### Step 3 - Convert to percentage

```text
3/4 = 75%
```

Answer: **75%** of the memory is used.
