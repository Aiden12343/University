# 3.1 Coordinates and Axes - Worked Examples

Theory note: [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes]]

Use these before [[3.1 Coordinates and Axes - Practice Questions]].

> [!tip] Work one step per line.

## Example 1
Question: Plot (3, -2)

### Steps
Start at origin. Right 3, down 2. Quadrant IV.

Answer: **Quadrant IV**

## Example 2
Question: Quadrant of (-4, 1)

### Steps
x negative, y positive → Quadrant II

Answer: **Quadrant II**

## Example 3
Question: Write coords: 5 left, 3 down

### Steps
x=-5, y=-3

Answer: **(-5, -3)**

## Example 4
Question: Spot mistake: swap x,y

### Steps
First number is x (horizontal)

Answer: **Swapped x and y**

## Example 5
Question: Point on y-axis (0, -6)

### Steps
x=0 means on y-axis, not in quadrant

Answer: **On y-axis**

## Example 6
Question: CS: pixel (120, 80)

### Steps
Near left, near top if y-down

Answer: **Top-left region**
