# 4.2 Perimeter Area and Volume - Worked Examples

Theory note: [[4.2 Perimeter Area and Volume]]

Use these examples before attempting [[4.2 Perimeter Area and Volume - Practice Questions]].

> [!tip] Worked example routine
> Sketch the shape, label edges, write the formula (P, A, or V), substitute, then check units before finishing.

## Example 1 - Rectangle Perimeter and Area
Question: A monitor panel is 24 cm wide and 13 cm tall. Find perimeter and area.

### Step 1 - Perimeter
```text
P = 2(24) + 2(13)
= 48 + 26
= 74 cm
```

### Step 2 - Area
```text
A = 24 × 13
= 312 cm²
```

Answer: **P = 74 cm**, **A = 312 cm²**.

## Example 2 - Triangle Area
Question: Find the area of a triangle with base 14 m and height 9 m.

### Step 1 - Write formula
```text
A = ½ × b × h
```

### Step 2 - Substitute
```text
A = ½ × 14 × 9
= 7 × 9
= 63 m²
```

Answer: **63 m²**.

## Example 3 - Cuboid Volume
Question: A shipping crate is 1.2 m × 0.8 m × 0.5 m. Find its volume.

### Step 1 - Multiply three edges
```text
V = 1.2 × 0.8 × 0.5
```

### Step 2 - Calculate
```text
V = 0.48 m³
```

Answer: **0.48 m³**.

## Example 4 - CS Transfer: Texture Memory
Question: A 1920×1080 RGBA texture uses 4 bytes per pixel. How many bytes of memory?

### Step 1 - Pixel area
```text
1920 × 1080 = 2,073,600 pixels
```

### Step 2 - Bytes per pixel
```text
2,073,600 × 4 = 8,294,400 bytes
```

### Step 3 - Interpret
```text
≈ 7.9 MiB (8,294,400 / 1,048,576)
```

Answer: **8,294,400 bytes**.

## Example 5 - Composite L-Shape
Question: An L-shape is a 10 cm × 6 cm rectangle with a 4 cm × 3 cm corner cut out. Find the area.

### Step 1 - Full rectangle
```text
10 × 6 = 60 cm²
```

### Step 2 - Removed corner
```text
4 × 3 = 12 cm²
```

### Step 3 - Subtract
```text
60 - 12 = 48 cm²
```

Answer: **48 cm²**.

## Example 6 - Spot the Mistake
Question: A learner says a 5 cm × 5 cm square has area 20 cm. Identify and fix the error.

### Step 1 - Identify mistake
```text
The learner computed perimeter: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 20
But the question asked for area.
```

### Step 2 - Correct calculation
```text
A = 5 × 5 = 25 cm²
```

### Step 3 - Explain
```text
Perimeter uses length units (cm).
Area uses squared units (cm²).
```

Answer: **Area is 25 cm²**, not 20 cm.
