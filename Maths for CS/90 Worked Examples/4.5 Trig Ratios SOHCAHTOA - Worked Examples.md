# 4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA - Worked Examples

Theory note: [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA]]

Use these examples before attempting [[4.5 Trig Ratios SOHCAHTOA - Practice Questions]].

> [!tip] How to use worked examples
> For each example, draw the triangle yourself before reading the solution. Label opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse relative to the marked angle.

## Example 1 - Labelling the Triangle
Question: In a right triangle with angle θ at the bottom-left, label opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse.

### Step 1 - Identify the right angle
The right angle is at the bottom-right. The side opposite it is the hypotenuse.

### Step 2 - Locate angle θ
θ is at the bottom-left corner.

### Step 3 - Label sides relative to θ
- **Hypotenuse**: longest side, opposite the right angle
- **Opposite**: side across from θ (the vertical leg)
- **Adjacent**: side next to θ (the horizontal leg)

```text
         /|
        / |
   hyp /  | opp (opposite θ)
      /   |
     / θ  |
    /_____|
      adj (adjacent to θ)
```

Answer: Label as shown above.

## Example 2 - Find sin, cos, tan Given Sides
Question: A right triangle has sides: opposite = 5, adjacent = 12, hypotenuse = 13. Find sin θ, cos θ, and tan θ.

### Step 1 - Apply SOHCAHTOA
sin θ = O/H = 5/13.
cos θ = A/H = 12/13.
tan θ = O/A = 5/12.

### Step 2 - Simplify if needed
All three are already in simplest form.

Answer: sin θ = **5/13**, cos θ = **12/13**, tan θ = **5/12**.

## Example 3 - Find a Side Using Trig
Question: A right triangle has angle 30° and hypotenuse 10 cm. Find the opposite side.

### Step 1 - Choose the ratio linking O and H
SOH: sin θ = O/H.

### Step 2 - Substitute
sin 30° = O/10.

### Step 3 - sin 30° = 0.5
0.5 = O/10.

### Step 4 - Multiply both sides by 10
O = 5.

Answer: The opposite side is **5 cm**.

## Example 4 - Find the Angle Using Inverse Trig
Question: A right triangle has opposite = 4 and adjacent = 3. Find the angle θ.

### Step 1 - Choose the ratio linking O and A
TOA: tan θ = O/A = 4/3.

### Step 2 - Use inverse tan
θ = tan⁻¹(4/3).

### Step 3 - Compute
θ ≈ 53.13°.

Answer: θ ≈ **53.13°**.

## Example 5 - Spot the Mistake
Question: A student solves for angle θ where opposite = 8, hypotenuse = 10. They write: cos θ = 8/10 = 0.8, so θ = cos⁻¹(0.8) ≈ 36.9°. What is wrong?

### Step 1 - Identify the ratio used
cos θ = A/H, but the student used opposite/hypotenuse.

### Step 2 - The correct ratio is sin
sin θ = O/H = 8/10 = 0.8.

### Step 3 - Correct calculation
θ = sin⁻¹(0.8) ≈ 53.13°.

Answer: The student used cosine instead of sine. The correct answer is **θ ≈ 53.13°**.

## Example 6 - CS Transfer: Aiming in a Game
Question: A game character at (0, 0) aims at a target at (12, 5). What angle (from horizontal) must the character face?

### Step 1 - Draw the triangle
Horizontal leg = 12 (adjacent to angle). Vertical leg = 5 (opposite).

### Step 2 - Choose the ratio
tan θ = O/A = 5/12.

### Step 3 - Use inverse tan
θ = tan⁻¹(5/12) ≈ 22.62°.

Answer: The character must face at approximately **22.62°** above horizontal.
