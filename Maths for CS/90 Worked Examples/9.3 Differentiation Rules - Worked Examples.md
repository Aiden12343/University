## Example 1 - Vocabulary in Context
**Question:** Explain differentiation rules using [[power rule]], [[product rule]], and [[quotient rule]].

**Worked solution:**
1. A [[power rule]] is: for f(x)=x^n, the derivative is n·x^(n-1).
2. [[product rule]] means: the derivative of u·v is u'·v + u·v'.
3. [[quotient rule]] means: the derivative of u/v is (u'·v − u·v') / v².
