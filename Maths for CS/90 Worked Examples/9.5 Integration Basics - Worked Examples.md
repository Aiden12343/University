## Example 1 - Vocabulary in Context
**Question:** Explain integration basics using [[integral]], [[area under curve]], and [[antiderivative]].

**Worked solution:**
1. A [[integral]] is: the accumulated sum of infinitely many tiny contributions, written ∫f(x)dx.
2. [[area under curve]] means: the geometric interpretation of a definite integral between two x-values.
3. [[antiderivative]] means: a function F whose derivative is f; also called an indefinite integral.
4. Together, these ideas let you integration reverses differentiation. it measures **accumulation** — total area ...
5. This matters because integration appears in probability, physics engines, and continuous models.
