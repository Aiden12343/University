## Example 1 - Vocabulary in Context
**Question:** Explain differential equations intro using [[differential equation]], [[initial condition]], and [[solution curve]].

**Worked solution:**
1. A [[differential equation]] is: an equation involving a function and one or more of its derivatives.
2. [[initial condition]] means: a known value at a starting time or position that picks one solution from a family.
3. [[solution curve]] means: the function y(t) or y(x) that satisfies the differential equation everywhere in its domain.
