# 1.1 Counting and Number Sense - Practice Questions

Theory note: [[1.1 Counting and Number Sense]]

Answers: [[1.1 Counting and Number Sense - Answers]]

> [!warning] Practice rule
> Try every question on paper before checking the answers. If you only read the answers, you are training recognition, not recall.

## A. Vocabulary
1. What question does counting answer?
2. What does [[one-to-one matching]] mean?
3. What does [[zero as a number]] mean?
4. In your own words, explain `less than`.
5. In your own words, explain `greater than`.
6. What is a [[number line]]?

## B. Counting Objects
7. Count the stars:

```text
* * * * *
```

8. Count the boxes:

```text
[] [] [] [] [] [] []
```

9. Count the dots:

```text
. . .
```

10. How many triangles are shown?

```text
^ ^ ^ ^
```

11. How many circles are shown?

```text
o o o o o o
```

## C. Spot the Mistake
12. What is wrong with this count?

```text
[] [] [] []
1  2  2  3
```

13. What is wrong with this count?

```text
* * * * *
1 2 3 4
```

14. What is wrong with this count?

```text
o o o
1 3 4
```

15. Correct this count:

```text
[] [] [] [] [] []
1  2  3  5  6  7
```

## D. Comparing Numbers
Fill in `<`, `>`, or `=`.

16. `3 __ 5`
17. `9 __ 2`
18. `4 __ 4`
19. `0 __ 1`
20. `8 __ 6`
21. `7 __ 10`
22. `12 __ 12`

## E. Number Lines
23. Draw a number line from 0 to 8.
24. On your number line, mark 3 and 7.
25. Which is further right: 3 or 7?
26. Which is smaller: 3 or 7?
27. Explain why numbers to the right are greater on this number line.

## F. Counting On
28. Start at 4 and count on 3 more.
29. Start at 8 and count on 2 more.
30. You have 5 books and get 4 more. Count on to find the total.
31. You have 7 coins and get 1 more. Count on to find the total.

## G. Computer Science Transfer
32. A list has 3 items and starts indexing at 0. Write the indexes.
33. A list has 6 items and starts indexing at 0. What is the final index?
34. A loop prints the numbers `0, 1, 2, 3`. How many numbers did it print?
35. Explain why counting carefully matters when writing loops.

## H. Explain It
36. Explain why the final count word gives the total.
37. Explain why zero is useful in computer science.
38. Make up your own counting question with 5 to 10 objects.
39. Solve your own question.
40. Write one mistake someone might make when solving your question.

Pass target: 90%. You should get at least 36 out of 40 correct before taking [[1.1 Counting and Number Sense - Mastery Test]].
