Do these on paper first. Check [[10.12 Research Paper Maths Reading - Answers]] only after making a serious attempt.

## Vocabulary
1. Define definition.
2. Define lemma.
3. Define theorem.
4. Write one sentence explaining the difference between two of the new words.

## Core Skill
5. You have 15 papers on a literature review reading list. Apply the three-pass strategy: you reject 9 after the first pass and deep-dive the 2 most important. How many papers reach each stage, and what is the total estimated time?

6. Interpret the following theorem: "Theorem: For any ε > 0, with probability at least 1 − δ, the generalisation error of hypothesis h is bounded by Ê(h) + O(√(d/n) + √(log(1/δ)/n))." Identify the assumptions, the conclusion, and what "with high probability" means.

7. A paper says: "By standard convex optimisation analysis (see Appendix), SGD with step size ηₜ = 1/t achieves O(1/√T) regret." Using the result f(w_T) − f(w*) ≤ L||w₀ − w*||² / (2ηT), explain what changes when the step size decreases over time rather than staying constant.

8. Build a notation dictionary from this excerpt: "Let xᵢ ∈ ℝᵈ be input features, yᵢ ∈ {0,1} binary labels. The classifier f_w(x) = σ(w·x) with sigmoid σ(z) = 1/(1 + e⁻ᶻ) minimises cross-entropy loss L(w) = −∑[yᵢ log f_w(xᵢ) + (1 − yᵢ) log(1 − f_w(xᵢ))]." List each symbol and its meaning.

## Error Checking
9. A student reads a research paper by starting at page 1 and reading line-by-line like a textbook. After 4 hours they are still on page 3 of a 10-page paper. Identify the mistake and describe the correct three-pass strategy.

10. Verify the gradient descent claim: from the L-smoothness inequality f(y) ≤ f(x) + ∇f(x)ᵀ(y − x) + (L/2)||y − x||², substitute y = w_{t+1} = w_t − η∇f(w_t) and x = w_t, and show that when η ≤ 1/L we get f(w_{t+1}) ≤ f(w_t) − (η/2)||∇f(w_t)||².

## CS Transfer
11. You are tasked with implementing a paper's core algorithm. Explain how running the author's published code helps clarify ambiguous notation, and what steps you would take if no code is provided.

12. Write a one-paragraph answer explaining the three-pass reading strategy and why it is more effective than reading a research paper linearly from start to finish.

Pass target: 85%. Definitions must be perfect.
