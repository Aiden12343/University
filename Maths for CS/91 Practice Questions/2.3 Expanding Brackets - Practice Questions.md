## A. Vocabulary
1. State the [[distributive law]] in symbols.
2. What is a [[single bracket]]? Give an example.
3. What is a [[double bracket]]? Give an example.
4. What is the [[area model]] used for?
5. Why is [[sign handling]] important when expanding?
6. Expand `2(x + 1)` as a check that you understand the distributive law.

## B. Core Skill — Single Brackets
7. Expand `3(x + 4)`.
8. Expand `5(2x - 1)`.
9. Expand `-2(x + 3)`.
10. Expand `-(x - 5)`.
11. Expand `4(x + 2y - 1)`.
12. Expand and simplify `2(x + 3) + 5`.
13. Expand and simplify `3(x - 2) - 2(x + 1)`.
14. Expand `-4(2 - x)`.
15. Expand `x(x + 3)`.

## C. Core Skill — Double Brackets
16. Expand `(x + 1)(x + 4)`.
17. Expand `(x + 3)(x + 2)`.
18. Expand `(x - 2)(x + 5)`.
19. Expand `(x + 7)(x - 1)`.
20. Expand `(x - 3)(x - 2)`.
21. Draw the area model for `(x + 2)(x + 3)` and write the expanded form.

## D. Spot the Mistake
22. A learner writes `5(x + 2) = 5x + 2`. Correct it.
23. A learner writes `-3(x - 1) = -3x - 3`. Correct it.
24. A learner writes `(x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 6`. What two errors were made?
25. A learner writes `-(x + 4) = -x + 4`. Correct it.

## E. Mixed
26. Expand and simplify `2(x + 1) + 3(x + 2)`.
27. Expand and simplify `4(x - 1) - 2(x + 3)`.
28. Expand `(x + 2)(x + 2)` and simplify.
29. If `x = 2`, evaluate `3(x + 1)` before and after expanding. Do the results match?
30. Explain in one sentence why expanding and simplifying are often done together.

## F. Computer Science Transfer
31. Rewrite `scale * (r + g + b)` in expanded form, like loop unrolling.
32. Why might a compiler unroll a small loop instead of keeping the compact loop form?
33. In SymPy, `expand(2*(x + 3))` gives `2*x + 6`. What mathematical rule did SymPy apply?
34. Give one situation where expanding an expression before simplifying helps a compiler.
35. Write one sentence explaining why the distributive law belongs in a Computer Science maths course.

Pass target: 90%. You should get at least 32 out of 35 correct before taking [[2.3 Expanding Brackets - Mastery Test]].
