# 2.6 Rearranging Formulae - Practice Questions

Theory note: [[2.6 Rearranging Formulae]]

Answers: [[2.6 Rearranging Formulae - Answers]]

> [!warning] Practice rule
> Write one operation per line. After rearranging, substitute test values and check in the original formula before looking at the answers.

## A. Vocabulary
1. What is a [[formula]]?
2. What is the [[subject]] of a formula?
3. What does it mean to [[isolate]] a letter?
4. What is an [[inverse operation]]?
5. What does [[substitute]] mean?
6. In one sentence, explain how rearranging a formula relates to [[2.5 Solving Linear Equations]].

## B. One-Step Rearrangements
7. Make `w` the subject of `A = l × w`.
8. Make `t` the subject of `d = s × t`.
9. Make `s` the subject of `d = s × t`.
10. Make `m` the subject of `F = m × a`.
11. Make `a` the subject of `F = m × a`.
12. Make `b` the subject of `A = b × h`.
13. Make `h` the subject of `A = b × h`.
14. Make `r` the subject of `C = 2 × π × r`. (Leave π as π.)

## C. Two-Step Rearrangements
15. Make `u` the subject of `v = u + at`.
16. Make `t` the subject of `v = u + at`.
17. Make `x` the subject of `y = 2x + 1`.
18. Make `x` the subject of `y = 3x - 4`.
19. Make `x` the subject of `y = 5x + 2`.
20. Make `C` the subject of `F = (9/5) × C + 32`.

## D. Substitute and Check
21. Using `t = d/s`, find `t` when `d = 120` and `s = 40`. Check in `d = s × t`.
22. Using `u = v - at`, find `u` when `v = 30`, `a = 2`, `t = 5`. Check in `v = u + at`.
23. Using `x = (y - 3)/2`, find `x` when `y = 11`. Check in `y = 2x + 3`.
24. Using `w = A/l`, find `w` when `A = 45` and `l = 9`. Check in `A = l × w`.
25. Using `s = d/t`, find `s` when `d = 200` and `t = 8`. Check in `d = s × t`.

## E. Spot the Mistake
26. A learner writes `y = 2x + 3` → `x = y/2 + 3`. Explain the error and give the correct subject form.
27. A learner writes `v = u + at` → `u = v + at`. Explain the sign error and correct it.
28. A learner writes `d = s × t` → `s = d × t`. Explain why this is wrong.
29. A learner rearranges correctly but checks using the rearranged formula only, not the original. Why is that risky?
30. A learner substitutes `d = 100`, `s = 0` into `t = d/s`. What mathematical problem arises?

## F. Computer Science Transfer
31. Write a Python function `celsius_from_fahrenheit(F)` using `C = (F - 32) × 5/9`.
32. Given `pixels = dpi × inches`, write a formula for `inches` and a one-line Python expression.
33. Given `total_time = count × time_per_item`, write a formula for `time_per_item`.
34. Explain why implementing `x = y/2 + 3` instead of `x = (y - 3)/2` would be a bug.
35. Write one sentence explaining why programmers need to rearrange formulas, not just memorise one direction.

Pass target: 90%. You should get at least 32 out of 35 correct before taking [[2.6 Rearranging Formulae - Mastery Test]].
