## D. Mixed and Mistakes
17. Solve: `3x + y = 14`, `x - y = 2`.
18. Solve: `2x + 3y = 21`, `4x - y = 11`.
19. Is `(1, 4)` a solution to `x + 2y = 9` and `3x - y = -1`?
20. Is `(3, 2)` a solution to `2x + y = 8` and `x - y = 2`?
21. Correct the wrong answer: a learner says `x + y = 6` and `x - y = 2` gives `x = 3`, `y = 2`.
22. A learner subtracts `x + y = 5` from `2x + y = 8` and writes `x = 3`. What is `y`?
23. How many solutions: `x + 2y = 6` and `2x + 4y = 12`?
24. How many solutions: `x + y = 4` and `x + y = 9`?

## E. Mixed Applications
25. Two numbers add to 50 and differ by 8. Find both numbers.
26. The sum of two digits-related numbers is 17; their difference is 3. Find them.
27. Tickets cost £4 (child) and £7 (adult). Three children and two adults cost £26. Write one equation only for this purchase.
28. Using question 27, if two purchases give `4c + 7a = 26` and `3c + 2a = 19`, find `c` and `a`.
29. Lines `y = 2x + 1` and `y = -x + 7` intersect where?
30. Lines `y = 3x` and `y = 3x + 2` — how many intersection points?

## F. Computer Science Transfer
31. A buffer must satisfy `readers + writers = 10` and `readers - writers = 4`. Find both counts.
32. A rectangle has `width + height = 120` and `width = height + 20`. Find both.
33. Two network flows satisfy `f1 + f2 = 100` and `f1 - f2 = 20`. Find `f1` and `f2`.
34. Explain why inconsistent simultaneous equations might appear in a software specification.
35. Write a 2×2 system that models choosing `x` cheap plans and `y` premium plans when total plans = 8 and total cost = £52, with costs £4 and £10.

Pass target: 90%. You should get at least 32 out of 35 correct before taking [[2.8 Simultaneous Equations - Mastery Test]].
