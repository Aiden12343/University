## A. Vocabulary
1. What is model space?
2. What is world space?
3. What is screen space?
4. What does translation do to coordinates?
5. What does scaling do to coordinates?
6. What does rotation preserve that scaling does not?

## B. Translation
7. Translate (2, 3) by vector (5, 1).
8. A triangle (0,0), (2,0), (1,3) is translated by (3, 2). Find new vertices.
9. A 3D point (1, 2, 3) is translated by (10, 0, 5). Where is it?

## C. Scaling
10. Scale (2, 3) by factor 4.
11. A square (0,0), (2,0), (2,2), (0,2) is scaled by 3. Find new corners.
12. A 3D point (1, 1, 1) is scaled by factor 5. Where is it?

## D. Rotation
13. Rotate (5, 0) by 90°. cos 90° = 0, sin 90° = 1.
14. Rotate (0, 3) by 180°. cos 180° = -1, sin 180° = 0.
15. Rotate (4, 0) by 60°. cos 60° = 0.5, sin 60° = √3/2 (approximate).

## E. Spot the Mistake
16. Student translates (2, 3) by (4, 1) and gets (6, 4). Is this correct?
17. Student scales (3, 4) by 2 and gets (5, 6). What is wrong?
18. Student rotates (4, 0) by 90° and gets (0, -4). What is wrong? (Correct is (0, 4).)

## F. CS Transfer
19. A model vertex at (3, 0, 0) is in model space. The model is placed at world (10, 5, 0). Where in world space?
20. A camera at (0, 0, -10) views an object at (5, 3, 20). What is the camera-space z-coordinate?
21. Normal N = (0, 1), light L = (1, 1) normalised. Compute diffuse.
22. A button on screen has centre (100, 200) and size (80, 30). If the screen is scaled 2×, what is the new size?
23. Explain why rotation preserves distance between points.
24. A game object rotates 45° per second. After 2 seconds, what is the total rotation in degrees? In radians?
25. In the Phong model, what does the dot product L·N compute?
