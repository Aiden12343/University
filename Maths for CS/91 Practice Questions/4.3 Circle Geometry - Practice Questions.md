# 4.3 Circle Geometry - Practice Questions

Theory note: [[4.3 Circle Geometry]]

Answers: [[4.3 Circle Geometry - Answers]]

> [!warning] Practice rule
> Try every question on paper before checking the answers. Drawing the circle helps — sketch it first.

## A. Vocabulary
1. What is the radius of a circle?
2. What is the diameter of a circle?
3. What is the circumference?
4. What does π represent?
5. What is an arc?
6. Write the relationship between radius and diameter.

## B. Core Skills
7. A circle has radius 11 cm. Find its diameter.
8. A circle has diameter 30 cm. Find its radius.
9. A circle has radius 8 cm. Find its circumference (π ≈ 3.14).
10. A circle has diameter 14 cm. Find its circumference (π ≈ 3.14).
11. A circle has radius 3 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
12. A circle has diameter 20 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
13. A circle has radius 9 cm and central angle 40°. Find the arc length (π ≈ 3.14).
14. A circle has radius 6 cm and central angle 45°. Find the sector area (π ≈ 3.14).
15. A tangent touches a circle of radius 7 cm. What is the angle between the radius and tangent at the point of tangency?

## C. Spot the Mistake
16. A student says: "A = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 5 = 31.4 cm² for a radius 5 circle." What is wrong?
17. A student says: "C = πr² = 3.14 × 36 = 113.04 for a radius 6 circle." What is wrong?
18. A student says: "The diameter is half the radius." Is this correct? If not, fix it.
19. A student says: "Arc length = (θ/360) × πr" but the formula should be (θ/360) × 2πr. Explain why.
20. A student says a chord and a diameter are the same thing. Are they?

## D. Mixed Problems
21. A circle has area 78.5 cm². Find its radius (π ≈ 3.14).
22. A circle has circumference 62.8 cm. Find its diameter (π ≈ 3.14).
23. A wheel of radius 35 cm makes 100 rotations. How far does it travel? (π ≈ 3.14)
24. A sector has angle 90° and radius 8 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
25. A circle fits exactly inside a square of side 10 cm. What is the circle's area? (π ≈ 3.14)
26. A circle has radius r. If you double the radius, what happens to the area?
27. A pizza has diameter 30 cm. It is cut into 8 equal slices. What is the area of one slice? (π ≈ 3.14)

## E. CS Transfer
28. A game has a circular hitbox with centre (0, 0) and radius 10. A projectile at (6, 8) is distance √(6² + 8²) from centre. Is it inside the hitbox?
29. Explain why circular collision detection is faster than rectangular collision detection for rotation.
30. A robot wheel has radius 5 cm. How many rotations to travel 314 cm? (π ≈ 3.14)
31. In Bresenham's circle algorithm, only integer arithmetic is used. Why is this important for embedded systems?
32. A radar displays objects within 50 km of centre (0, 0). An object is at (30, 40). Is it within range?

Pass target: 90%. Get at least 29 out of 32 correct before taking [[4.3 Circle Geometry - Mastery Test]].
