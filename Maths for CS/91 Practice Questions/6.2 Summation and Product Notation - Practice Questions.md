# 6.2 Summation and Product Notation - Practice Questions

Theory note: [[6.2 Summation and Product Notation]]

Do these on paper first. Check [[6.2 Summation and Product Notation - Answers]] only after making a serious attempt.

## Vocabulary
1. Define sigma notation.
2. Define product notation.
3. Define index.
4. Write one sentence explaining the difference between two of the new words.

## Core Skill
5. Expand and compute Σᵢ₌₁⁵ (2i + 3) by writing out all terms. Then recompute using the properties (constant factor rule and sum splitting rule) to verify.

6. Use the closed form Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ i² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 to compute Σᵢ₌₁¹⁰ (3i²). Then compute Πⱼ₌₁⁴ (j + 2) by expanding and multiplying.

7. Compute the double sum Σᵢ₌₁³ Σⱼ₌₁⁴ (i + j) by expanding the outer sum: for each i, expand the inner sum over j, then add the results.

8. Using the properties of sigma notation and the closed forms for Σi and Σi², compute Σᵢ₌₁⁶ (3i² − 2i + 5).

## Error Checking
9. A student writes: Σᵢ₌₁⁴ i² = (Σᵢ₌₁⁴ i) × (Σᵢ₌₁⁴ i) = 10 × 10 = 100. Identify the mistake and give the correct value.

10. Compute Σᵢ₌₁⁴ (2i + 1) by expanding term-by-term. Then compute the same sum using the closed forms Σi = n(n+1)/2 and Σc = n·c. Verify the two results match.

## CS Transfer
11. A nested loop runs for i = 1 to n and j = 1 to i, performing one print statement per iteration. Express the total number of print statements using sigma notation and give the closed form in terms of n.

12. Explain why a Σ sum corresponds to a for-loop with an accumulator, while a Π product corresponds to a for-loop with a product accumulator. Why is there no simple closed-form polynomial formula for Πᵢ₌₁ⁿ i = n! ?

Pass target: 85%. Definitions must be perfect.
