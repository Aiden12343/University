# 7.9 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors - Practice Questions

Theory note: [[7.9 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors]]

Do these on paper first. Check [[7.9 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors - Answers]] only after making a serious attempt.

## Vocabulary
1. Define eigenvector.
2. Define eigenvalue.
3. Define characteristic equation.
4. Write one sentence explaining the difference between two of the new words.

## Core Skill
5. Given A = [[1, 2], [2, 1]], verify that v = [1, 1]ᵀ is an eigenvector and find its eigenvalue λ.
6. Find the eigenvalues of A = [[2, -1], [-1, 2]] by solving det(A - λI) = 0.
7. For A = [[2, -1], [-1, 2]] with eigenvalues λ = 3 and λ = 1, find an eigenvector for each eigenvalue by solving (A - λI)v = 0.
8. Diagonalize A = [[2, -1], [-1, 2]] by forming P from the eigenvectors and D from the eigenvalues, then compute P⁻¹ and verify A = PDP⁻¹.

## Error Checking
9. A student computes the characteristic polynomial of [[4, 1], [2, 3]] as det([[4-λ, 1], [2, 3]]) - λ = (4-λ)(3) - 1×2 - λ = 12 - 3λ - 2 - λ = 10 - 4λ, and concludes λ = 2.5. Identify every mistake and write the correct characteristic polynomial.
10. A classmate claims that [0, 0]ᵀ is an eigenvector because A × [0, 0]ᵀ = λ × [0, 0]ᵀ for any λ. Identify whether this is correct and explain why eigenvectors are defined to be non-zero.

## CS Transfer
11. In PCA (Principal Component Analysis), the covariance matrix Σ = [[4, 2], [2, 3]] of a dataset is computed. Find its eigenvalues. If you want to reduce dimensionality from 2D to 1D, which eigenvector would you project onto, and why?
12. In PageRank, the web graph's adjacency matrix has a dominant eigenvector (with eigenvalue 1) whose entries represent page importance scores. Explain in your own words why the "direction-preserving" property Av = λv makes eigenvectors useful for ranking — what does it mean for a page's importance to be proportional to the sum of importances of pages linking to it?
