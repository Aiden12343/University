# 8.1 Probability Basics - Practice Questions

Theory note: [[8.1 Probability Basics]]

Do these on paper first. Check [[8.1 Probability Basics - Answers]] only after making a serious attempt.

## Vocabulary
1. Define event.
2. Define outcome.
3. Define sample space.
4. Write one sentence explaining the difference between two of the new words.

## Core Skill
5. A bag has 5 red marbles and 3 blue marbles. You pick one at random. Write down the sample space and compute P(red).

6. You flip a fair coin 4 times. Use the complement rule to compute P(at least one head).

7. P(sunny) = 0.4, P(windy) = 0.3, P(sunny AND windy) = 0.1. Use the addition rule to find P(sunny or windy).

8. A bag has 4 red and 2 blue marbles. Draw two WITHOUT replacement. Draw a probability tree and compute P(red then blue).

## Error Checking
9. A student writes: "P(roll a 3 on a fair die) = 1/6, P(roll a 5) = 1/6, so P(3 or 5) = 1/6 + 1/6 - 1/36 = 11/36." Identify the exact mistake and give the correct answer.

10. Compute P(at least one head in 2 fair coin flips) using the complement rule. Then verify by listing all 4 outcomes in the sample space and counting favourable ones.

## CS Transfer
11. A spam filter correctly flags 95% of spam as spam, but falsely flags 2% of legitimate email as spam. If 20% of all email is spam, use the law of total probability to compute the overall probability that an email is flagged as spam.

12. Explain why the law of total probability can be thought of as "summing over all paths in a probability tree." Give a one-sentence CS example where this is useful.

Pass target: 85%. Definitions must be perfect.
