
```text

+-------+
```

```text
o o o o
1 2 2 3
```

## Section D - Number Line and Counting On

17. Mark 4 and 9 on the number line.
18. Which is greater, 4 or 9?

20. You have 8 cards and get 2 more. Use counting on to find the total.
