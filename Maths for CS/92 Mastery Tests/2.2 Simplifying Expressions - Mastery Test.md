## Section B - Identify and Simplify

## Section C - Mistakes and Checking
12. A learner writes `5x - 3x = 2`. Correct the mistake and explain it.
13. Can `2x + 3y` be simplified to one term? Explain why or why not.
14. Check whether `5x + 2 - x + 3` simplifies to `4x + 5` using `x = 4`.
15. Write one common mistake when simplifying and how to avoid it.

## Section D - Mixed
16. Simplify `x + 2x + 3x - 1`.
17. Simplify `7a + 2b - 3a + b - 4`.
18. Write an expression with four terms that simplifies to `2x + 6`.
19. Explain why grouping like terms before combining helps prevent errors.
20. If `3x + 5 = 20`, use your simplified form of `3x + 2x + 5` to explain what `x` must be.

## Section E - Computer Science Transfer
21. Draw the expression tree for `3x + 4x` before and after simplification.
22. What CS structure represents `2x + 3 + x` internally before simplification?
23. Why do compilers simplify expressions like `a + a` to `2*a`?
24. Give one example of where expression simplification appears in software.
25. Write one sentence explaining why collecting like terms matters in CS.
