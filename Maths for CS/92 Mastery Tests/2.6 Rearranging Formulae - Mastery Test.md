## Section A - Definitions
Each answer must be in your own words.

1. What is a [[formula]]?
2. What is the [[subject]] of a formula?
3. What does [[isolate]] mean?
4. What is an [[inverse operation]]?
5. What does [[substitute]] mean?
6. How is rearranging a formula related to solving an equation?

## Section B - Make the Subject
7. Make `t` the subject of `d = s × t`.
8. Make `u` the subject of `v = u + at`.
9. Make `x` the subject of `y = 4x + 3`.
10. Make `x` the subject of `y = 2x - 7`.
11. Make `m` the subject of `E = m × c²`. (Treat c² as one constant factor.)
12. Make `C` the subject of `F = (9/5)C + 32`.

## Section C - Substitute and Check
13. Using your answer to Q7, find `t` when `d = 180` and `s = 45`. Check in the original formula.
14. Using your answer to Q9, find `x` when `y = 19`. Check in `y = 4x + 3`.
15. Using `s = d/t`, find `s` when `d = 240` and `t = 8`. Check in `d = s × t`.

## Section D - Spot the Mistake
16. A learner rearranges `y = 3x + 2` as `x = y/3 + 2`. Explain the error and give the correct answer.
17. A learner writes `F = ma` → `m = F × a`. What is wrong?
18. A learner checks a rearrangement using only the new formula, not the original. Why is that insufficient?

## Section E - Computer Science Transfer
19. Write a Python function that computes `time = distance / speed` given `distance` and `speed`, after rearranging `distance = speed × time`.
20. A shader computes `y = m * x + c`. Write the expression for `x` given `y`, `m`, and `c`.
21. Explain why `(F - 32) * 5/9` is correct for Celsius from Fahrenheit, not `(F / 9) * 5 - 32`.
22. Write one sentence explaining why rearranging formulas belongs in a Computer Science maths course.
23. Create one correction note for any question you missed, or write "none missed".
