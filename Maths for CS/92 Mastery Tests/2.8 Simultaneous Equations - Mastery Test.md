## Section A - Definitions
Each answer must be in your own words.

1. What is a system of equations?
2. What is substitution?
3. What is elimination?
4. What is an intersection point?
5. What is a consistent system?

## Section B - Substitution
6. Solve: `y = x + 1`, `2x + y = 13`.
7. Solve: `x = 3y`, `x + 2y = 15`.
8. Solve: `y = 5 - 2x`, `3x + y = 9`.

## Section C - Elimination
9. Solve: `x + y = 11`, `x - y = 3`.
10. Solve: `3x + 2y = 14`, `3x - 2y = 2`.
11. Solve: `2x + 5y = 19`, `x + 5y = 11`.
12. Solve: `4x + 3y = 18`, `2x - 3y = 6`.

## Section D - Mixed and Interpretation
13. Is `(2, 3)` a solution to `x + y = 5` and `2x - y = 1`?
14. How many solutions: `x + 2y = 8` and `2x + 4y = 16`?
15. How many solutions: `3x + 6y = 9` and `x + 2y = 5`?
16. Find where `y = x + 2` meets `y = -x + 8`.
17. Correct the mistake: `4x + y = 14`, `2x - y = 4` solved as `x = 2`, `y = 5`.

## Section E - Computer Science Transfer
18. A panel satisfies `w + h = 100` and `w - h = 20`. Find `w` and `h`.
19. Two services handle `r + s = 500` requests total, with `r = 3s`. Find `r` and `s`.
20. Explain one CS situation where a system has no solution and why that matters.
21. Write a 2×2 system for: total items 12, total cost £84, with items costing £5 and £9.
22. Solve your system from question 21.
23. Why must both original equations be checked after solving?
24. When is elimination usually faster than substitution?
25. Write one sentence explaining why simultaneous equations belong in a Computer Science maths course.
