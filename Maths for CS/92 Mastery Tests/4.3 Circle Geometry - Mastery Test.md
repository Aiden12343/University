## Section A - Definitions
Each answer must be in your own words.

1. What is the radius of a circle?
2. What is the diameter of a circle?
3. What is the circumference?
4. What is π?
5. What is an arc?
6. What is a tangent?

## Section B - Core Calculations
7. A circle has radius 15 cm. Find its diameter.
8. A circle has diameter 36 cm. Find its radius.
9. A circle has radius 5 cm. Find its circumference (π ≈ 3.14).
10. A circle has diameter 16 cm. Find its circumference (π ≈ 3.14).

## Section C - Area
11. A circle has radius 7 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
12. A circle has diameter 12 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
13. A circle has area 314 cm². Find its radius (π ≈ 3.14).

## Section D - Arcs and Sectors
14. A circle has radius 12 cm and central angle 60°. Find the arc length (π ≈ 3.14).
15. A circle has radius 9 cm and central angle 120°. Find the sector area (π ≈ 3.14).
16. What is the angle between a radius and a tangent at the point of tangency?
17. A chord of length 16 cm is drawn in a circle of radius 10 cm. How far is the chord from the centre? (Hint: draw the radius to the chord midpoint.)

## Section E - Computer Science Transfer
18. A game object has a circular hitbox with centre (15, 20) and radius 8. A second object at (25, 26) has radius 5. Do they collide? Show your working.
19. A robot wheel has radius 4 cm. How many full rotations to travel 200 cm? (π ≈ 3.14)
20. Explain why comparing squared distances is faster than comparing actual distances in collision detection.
21. A radar has range 100 km. An aircraft is at (60, 80) km from the radar centre. Is it within range?
22. Write one sentence explaining why circle geometry matters for computer graphics.
23. A circular button on a screen has centre (200, 300) and radius 50. The mouse clicks at (220, 280). Was the button clicked?
24. A sprite of radius 30 is at (100, 100). An enemy of radius 20 is at (130, 140). Are they touching?
25. Explain why π is the same value for every circle.
