# 4.8 Vectors Basics - Mastery Test

Theory note: [[4.8 Vectors Basics]]

Answers: [[4.8 Vectors Basics - Answers]]

Pass mark: 90%. Full marks on Section A required.

> [!danger] Test condition
> Do without notes. Use paper.

## Section A - Definitions (6 marks)
1. What is a vector?
2. What is magnitude?
3. How do you compute |v| for v = (x, y)?
4. What is a unit vector?
5. How do you add two vectors?
6. What does multiplying a vector by -1 do?

## Section B - Vector Operations (4 marks)
7. Find vector from (2, 3) to (8, 11).
8. Find |v| for v = (6, 8).
9. a = (3, 1), b = (2, 5). Find a + b.
10. v = (2, 3). Find 5v.

## Section C - Unit Vectors and Magnitudes (4 marks)
11. Find unit vector of (5, 12).
12. Find |v| for v = (8, 15).
13. Find |v| for v = (3, 4, 12).
14. Find unit vector of (3, 4, 12).

## Section D - 3D Vectors (3 marks)
15. a = (1, 3, 5), b = (2, 4, 6). Find a + b.
16. Find |a| for a = (2, 2, 1).
17. Find vector from (1, 1, 1) to (5, 4, 13).

## Section E - CS Transfer (8 marks)
18. A character moves by (3, 4) per frame. Speed?
19. After 6 frames, starting from (10, 10), where is the character?
20. A spaceship at (0, 0) targets (8, 15). What unit direction to aim?
21. A force vector is (12, 5) N. Its magnitude?
22. A 3D camera at (1, 2, 3) looks at (5, 6, 15). What is the direction vector (not normalised)?
23. Explain why vectors are the foundation of 3D graphics.
24. A player moves 5 units right and 12 units up. Write as a vector and find speed.

## Marking
Total: 25 marks. Pass: 23/25 + 6/6 Section A.
