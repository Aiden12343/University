### A. Vocabulary
1. A common factor is a factor shared by two or more numbers.
2. HCF is the largest factor shared by two or more numbers.
3. A common multiple is a multiple shared by two or more numbers.
4. LCM is the smallest positive multiple shared by two or more numbers.
5. It means comparing prime factorisations to find shared factors or combined multiples.
