## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. Ratio notation compares quantities in order, such as `2:3`.
2. A unit rate is a rate per one unit, such as requests per second.
3. Direct proportion means quantities scale by the same factor.
4. It means multiplying all parts by the same factor.
5. Unit conversion rewrites a measurement in a different unit without changing the amount.
6. Units tell what a number measures and prevent incorrect comparisons.
