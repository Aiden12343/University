## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. A digit is one written number symbol, such as 0, 1, 2, or 9.
2. Value means what a digit is worth in its position.
3. Expanded form writes a number as the sum of its place-value parts.
4. A number line places numbers in order on a line, usually with distance preserved.
5. Rounding to a landmark means replacing a number with a nearby useful number, such as the nearest 10 or 100.
6. Compare by digit length first. If lengths match, compare digits from left to right.
