## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. Addition as combining means putting quantities together to find a total.
2. Subtraction as taking away means removing part of a quantity to find what remains.
3. Subtraction as difference means finding how far apart two quantities are.
4. Carrying means regrouping 10 or more ones as a ten, or 10 or more tens as a hundred, and so on.
5. Borrowing/regrouping means changing one higher-place unit into smaller-place units, such as 1 ten into 10 ones.
6. An inverse operation check uses the opposite operation to verify the answer.
