## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. Sharing equally means splitting a total so each person or group receives the same amount.
2. Grouping means making equal-size groups from a total and counting how many groups fit.
3. A remainder is what is left over after making as many equal groups as possible.
4. Division undoes multiplication. For example, `6 x 7 = 42`, so `42 / 7 = 6`.
5. Dividing by 10 moves digits one place-value column to the right.
6. If the remainder is at least as large as the divisor, another group can still be made.
