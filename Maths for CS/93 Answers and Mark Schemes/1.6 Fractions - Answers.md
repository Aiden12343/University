## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. The numerator tells how many parts are selected or counted.
2. The denominator tells how many equal parts make the whole.
3. Equivalent fractions have the same value even if they use different numbers.
4. Simplifying means rewriting a fraction as an equivalent fraction with smaller numbers.
5. Use a common denominator; if denominators already match, add numerators and keep the denominator.
6. Multiplying fractions often means finding a fraction of a fraction.
