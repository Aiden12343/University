## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. Tenths are parts made by splitting one whole into 10 equal parts.
2. Hundredths are parts made by splitting one whole into 100 equal parts.
3. Decimal place value is the value of digits after the decimal point.
4. Converting fractions means rewriting the same value as a fraction, decimal, or percentage.
5. Percent means per hundred, or out of 100.
6. A percentage increase adds a percentage of the original amount.
7. A percentage decrease subtracts a percentage of the original amount.
