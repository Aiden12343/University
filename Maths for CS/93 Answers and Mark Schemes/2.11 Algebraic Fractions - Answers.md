### D. Solve

### E. Mistakes and Mixed
26. Cannot cancel `(x^2+4)/2` that way; not valid simplification. (1 mark)
27. Must state `x ≠ 1`. (1 mark)
28. `(x - 3)/x`, `x ≠ 0, -3` (1 mark)
29. `x ≠ 2, -2` (1 mark)
30. `x + 3`, `x ≠ -3` (1 mark)

### F. CS Transfer
31. `s/(s+f)`, undefined when `s + f = 0`. (1 mark)
32. `2 + 1/n`, `n ≠ 0` (1 mark)
33. Division by zero is undefined. (1 mark)
34. Average undefined when count is zero. (1 mark)
35. `n - 1`, `n ≠ -1`; guard `n + 1 != 0`. (1 mark)

## Mark Scheme

- Mastery: 25 marks (1 each). Pass: 23/25 with 5/5 on Section A.
