## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. Balancing means keeping both sides of an equation equal when you add, subtract, multiply, or divide. (1 mark)
2. Inverse operations undo each other; for example, addition and subtraction, or multiplication and division. (1 mark)
3. A one-step equation can be solved with a single inverse operation. (1 mark)
4. A two-step equation needs two inverse operations in the correct order. (1 mark)
5. Unknown on both sides means the variable appears on the left and the right of the equals sign. (1 mark)
6. Both sides must stay equal; changing only one side breaks the balance. (1 mark)

## Mastery Test Answers

### Section A - Definitions
1. Balancing means keeping both sides of an equation equal when performing operations on both sides.
2. Inverse operations are pairs that undo each other, such as addition/subtraction or multiplication/division.
3. A one-step equation is solved with one inverse operation.
4. A two-step equation requires two inverse operations in reverse order of operations.
5. Unknown on both sides means the variable appears on both sides of the equals sign.
6. Both sides must remain equal; applying an operation to only one side breaks the equation.

### Section B - One-Step and Two-Step
7. `x = 7`. Check: `7 + 8 = 15`.
8. `n = 16`. Check: `16 - 4 = 12`.
9. `x = 7`. Check: `6 × 7 = 42`.
10. `2x = 10`, `x = 5`. Check: `2(5) + 3 = 13`.
11. `3x = 21`, `x = 7`. Check: `3(7) - 5 = 16`.
12. `4x = 20`, `x = 5`. Check: `4(5) + 1 = 21`.

### Section C - Unknown on Both Sides

### Section D - Spot the Mistake
17. They added 6 instead of subtracting. Correct: `x = 8`. Check: `8 + 6 = 14`.

## Common Wrong Answers
- `x + 5 = 12` → `x = 17`: added instead of subtracted. Fix: use inverse operation.
- `2x + 3 = 11` → `x = 7`: divided before subtracting. Fix: undo addition first.
- Checking `3x = 15` with `3 + 5 = 15`: confused multiplication notation with addition.
