## Practice Questions - Answers

### Section A - Vocabulary
1. A [[parabola]] is the U-shaped graph made by a quadratic function.
2. A [[turning point]] is where a curve changes from falling to rising, or rising to falling.
3. [[roots]] are x-values where the function equals zero.
4. The [[axis of symmetry]] is the vertical line that splits the parabola into two mirror-image halves.
5. The [[quadratic coefficient]] is the number multiplying x squared in `ax^2 + bx + c`.
6. The letter **a** tells you whether the parabola opens up or down.

### Section B - Core Skills

## Common Wrong Answers
- Writing only one root from factorised form: check both brackets.
- Calling the y-intercept a root: roots are where y = 0.
- Writing an axis as `(x, y)`: the axis is a line, written `x = ...`.
- Dividing by x in equations like `x^2 - 4x = 0`: factorise to keep x = 0.
