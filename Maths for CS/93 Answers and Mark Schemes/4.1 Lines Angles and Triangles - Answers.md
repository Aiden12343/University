## Practice Questions Answers

### A. Vocabulary (6 questions)
1. A [[line]] is a straight one-dimensional figure extending infinitely in both directions.
2. An [[angle]] is the amount of turn between two rays sharing a common endpoint (vertex), measured in degrees.
3. [[Parallel]] lines are lines in the same plane that never meet, no matter how far extended.
4. A [[triangle]] is a three-sided polygon whose interior angles always sum to 180°.
5. [[Angle sum]] is the total of all interior angles in a polygon; for a triangle it is 180°.
6. (i) line segment → C, (ii) ray → A, (iii) line → B.

### B. Core Skills (10 questions)
7. 12° acute, 95° obtuse, 180° straight, 250° reflex, 90° right.

8. 180° - 62° - 48° = 180° - 110° = **70°**.

9. (a) line segment, (b) line, (c) ray, (d) ray (pointing left).

10. Corresponding = 54°, alternate = 54°, co-interior = 180° - 54° = 126°.

11. All sides different → scalene. All angles < 90° → acute. **Scalene acute triangle**.

12. All sides equal → equilateral. All angles 60° → acute. **Equilateral acute triangle** (or just equilateral triangle).

13. All sides different → scalene. One angle 90° → right. **Scalene right triangle**.

14. P = 12 + 9 + 15 = **36 cm**.

15. P = 2(14) + 2(6) = 28 + 12 = **40 cm**.

16. Accept any correct hand-drawn diagram with vertex and rays labelled. Acute: any angle < 90°. Right: exactly 90° (square corner mark). Obtuse: between 90° and 180°.

### C. Spot the Mistake (5 questions)
17. The [[angle sum]] rule is exact, not approximate. 80 + 60 + 50 = 190, not 180. A triangle with these angles cannot exist. The correct sum of any triangle's angles is exactly 180°.

18. "Looks parallel" is not a proof. The lines in the diagram are not straight — they are curved. Parallel lines must be straight and in the same plane. You must be told $l \parallel m$ or prove it.

19. The [[angle]] measures the turn between the rays, not their length. A 30° angle stays 30° no matter how long you draw the rays. Side length and angle size are independent.

20. A triangle with sides 2, 2, 2 has all three sides equal → equilateral, not scalene. Scalene means all sides are different.

21. Incorrect. The correct rule: **alternate angles are equal**. Co-interior angles (not alternate) sum to 180°. The student confused the two rules.

### D. Mixed Problems (8 questions)
22. C = 180 - 37 - 53 = 90°. One angle is 90° → [[right triangle]].

23. 180 - 90 - 38 = **52°**.

24. Co-interior angles sum to 180°. 180 - 112 = **68°**.

25. Right angle (90°) + two equal sides → **isosceles right triangle**.

26. Two equal sides (8 = 8) → isosceles. One angle 92° > 90° → obtuse. **Isosceles obtuse triangle**.

27. a = 73° (given). b = 180 - 73 = 107° (adjacent on straight line). c = 73° (alternate to a). d = 107° (alternate to b). e = 73° (corresponding to a). f = 107° (corresponding to b). g = 73° (corresponding to c). h = 107° (corresponding to d).

28. P = a + b + c, so 30 = 11 + 9 + c, c = 30 - 20 = **10 cm**.

29. P = 2l + 2w, so 48 = 2l + 2(8), 48 = 2l + 16, 2l = 32, l = **16 cm**.

### E. CS Transfer (5 questions)
30. Three points always define a plane (triangles are always flat). Four points can be non-coplanar — one corner can lift out of the plane, creating a warped surface. GPUs render triangles because the planar guarantee means every pixel on the triangle surface has a well-defined depth.

31. The ray follows the x-axis in the positive direction: (0, 0, 0) → (1, 0, 0) → (2, 0, 0) → ... At x = 10, the ray continues to (11, 0, 0), (12, 0, 0), etc. It never stops — a ray extends infinitely in its direction.

32. 90° clockwise turn. Facing north, turning 90° clockwise → faces **east**.

33. 87° is less than 90° → [[acute angle]].

34. The sum of angles around a point is 360° (a full turn). This comes from the definition of an [[angle]] in Concept 2.

### Common Wrong Answers
- **Q7**: Confusing obtuse and reflex — 145° is obtuse (between 90 and 180), not reflex. 310° is reflex (between 180 and 360), not obtuse.
- **Q9**: Forgetting to show working — the answer 60° without steps gets only 1 mark.
- **Q12**: Getting the values right but not naming the angle relationships — the question explicitly asks for justification.
- **Q14**: Answering "because triangles are simpler" without explaining the planar property — needs the geometric reason.
