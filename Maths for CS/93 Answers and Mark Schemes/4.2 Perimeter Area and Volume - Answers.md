## Practice Questions — Full Solutions

### Section A — Vocabulary
1. **Perimeter:** total distance around the outside edge of a flat shape. (1)
2. **Area:** amount of flat surface inside a boundary, measured in square units. (1)
3. **Volume:** amount of three-dimensional space occupied, measured in cubic units. (1)
4. **Unit squared:** a square unit such as cm² used to measure area. (1)
5. **Unit cubed:** a cubic unit such as cm³ used to measure volume. (1)
6. Area spans two dimensions, so units are multiplied twice: unit × unit. (1)
