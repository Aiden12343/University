## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. The radius is the distance from the centre to any point on the edge of the circle.
2. The diameter is the distance across the circle through the centre.
3. The circumference is the total distance around the edge of the circle.
4. π is the constant ratio of circumference to diameter, approximately 3.14.
5. An arc is a portion of the circumference between two points on the circle.
6. d = 2r, or r = d/2.

### E. CS Transfer

29. Circular collision is rotation-invariant — a rotated circle is still a circle. Rectangular collision must check rotated axes, which is more complex.

31. Integer arithmetic is fast, deterministic, and does not need floating-point hardware — ideal for embedded systems with limited processors.

## Mastery Test Answers

### Section A
1. Radius: the distance from centre to edge of a circle.
2. Diameter: the distance across a circle through its centre.
3. Circumference: the total distance around a circle.
4. π: the ratio of circumference to diameter (≈ 3.14).
5. Arc: a portion of the circumference.
6. Tangent: a line that touches a circle at exactly one point.
