## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
1. The hypotenuse is the longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.
2. A right angle is an angle of exactly 90°.
3. a² means a multiplied by itself (a × a).
4. a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse.
5. d = √((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²)
6. If a² + b² = c² (where c is longest), the triangle is right-angled.
