Theory note: [[4.6 Unit Circle]]

## Practice Answers
### A. Vocabulary
1. A circle of radius 1 centred at the origin
2. (cos θ, sin θ)
3. The acute angle between the terminal arm and the x-axis
4. Quadrant I (all positive)
5. 360° (2π rad)
6. CAST: Cos (IV), All (I), Sin (II), Tan (III) — clockwise from Quadrant IV

### B. Coordinates
7. (1, 0)
8. (0, 1)
9. (√2/2, √2/2) ≈ (0.707, 0.707)
10. (√3/2, 1/2) ≈ (0.866, 0.5)
11. (1/2, √3/2) ≈ (0.5, 0.866)
12. 0° (or 360°)

### C. Angles > 90°
13. sin 120° = sin 60° = √3/2 (positive in QII)
14. cos 135° = -cos 45° = -√2/2
15. sin 150° = sin 30° = 1/2
16. cos 180° = -1
17. sin 210° = -sin 30° = -1/2
18. cos 270° = 0

### D. Quadrant Signs
19. Quadrant I
20. Quadrant IV
21. Quadrant II
22. Quadrant III
23. Quadrant III (tan positive in QIII, sin negative in QIII)

### E. Spot the Mistake
24. The student is correct. 150° has reference angle 30°, and sin is positive in QII, so sin 150° = sin 30° = 1/2.
25. No. 120° reference = 60°, cos is negative in QII, so cos 120° = -cos 60° = -1/2.

### F. CS Transfer
26. 360° or 2π rad
27. Frequency = 100 Hz, time = 0.01 s. Cycles = 100 × 0.01 = 1 cycle.
28. sin(2π × 50 × 0.005) = sin(0.5π) = sin(π/2) = 1
29. (x, y) → (-x, -y): negating both coordinates
30. The unit circle defines sin θ as the y-coordinate and cos θ as the x-coordinate of a point at angle θ, regardless of whether θ fits in a right triangle.
