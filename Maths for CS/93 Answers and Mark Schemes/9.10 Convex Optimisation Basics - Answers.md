## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
0. **[[convex function]]**: a function whose graph lies below every chord connecting two points on the curve.
1. **[[convex set]]**: a set where the line segment between any two points in the set stays inside the set.
2. **[[global minimum]]**: the lowest value of a function over its entire domain, not just locally.
3. **[[constraint]]**: a condition that limits which input values are allowed in an optimisation problem.
4. **[[Lagrange multiplier]]**: a scalar introduced to incorporate equality constraints into an unconstrained optimisation.
6. A **convex function** curves upward like a bowl — any local minimum is also the **global minimum**. **Convex sets** and **constraints** let you solve optimisation problems where gradient descent is guaranteed to succeed.
