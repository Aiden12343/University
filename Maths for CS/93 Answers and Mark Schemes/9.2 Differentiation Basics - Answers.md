## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
0. **[[derivative]]**: the instantaneous rate of change of a function with respect to its input.
1. **[[gradient function]]**: the rule f'(x) that gives the derivative at every point x in the domain.
2. **[[tangent]]**: a straight line that touches a curve at exactly one point locally and has the same slope as the curve there.
3. **[[rate of change]]**: how much the output increases or decreases per unit increase in the input.
4. **[[first principles]]**: finding a derivative directly from the limit definition before using shortcut rules.
6. The **derivative** measures how fast a function's output changes when the input changes by a tiny amount. It is the slope of the tangent line at a point — the **instantaneous rate of change**.
