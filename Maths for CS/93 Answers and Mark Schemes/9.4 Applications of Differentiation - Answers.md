## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
0. **[[turning point]]**: a point where a curve changes from increasing to decreasing or vice versa.
1. **[[maximum]]**: a point where the function value is greater than or equal to all nearby values.
2. **[[minimum]]**: a point where the function value is less than or equal to all nearby values.
3. **[[stationary point]]**: a point where the first derivative equals zero.
4. **[[second derivative]]**: the derivative of the derivative, used to classify stationary points as max, min, or inflection.
6. Derivatives tell you where a curve rises, falls, or turns. Setting the derivative to zero finds **stationary points** — candidates for **maxima** and **minima** that matter in optimisation.
