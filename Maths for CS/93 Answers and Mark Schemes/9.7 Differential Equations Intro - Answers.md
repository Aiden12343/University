## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
0. **[[differential equation]]**: an equation involving a function and one or more of its derivatives.
1. **[[initial condition]]**: a known value at a starting time or position that picks one solution from a family.
2. **[[solution curve]]**: the function y(t) or y(x) that satisfies the differential equation everywhere in its domain.
3. **[[growth model]]**: a differential equation where the rate of change is proportional to the current amount (dy/dt = ky).
4. **[[decay model]]**: a growth model with negative rate constant, modelling radioactive decay or cooling.
6. A **differential equation** relates a quantity to how fast it changes. Given an **initial condition**, you find the **solution curve** that models growth, decay, or motion over time.
