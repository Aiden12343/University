## Practice Answers

### A. Vocabulary
0. **[[partial derivative]]**: the derivative of a multivariable function with respect to one variable, treating others as constants.
1. **[[gradient vector]]**: the vector of all partial derivatives, pointing in the direction of steepest increase.
2. **[[level curve]]**: a curve where the function has a constant value, like contour lines on a map.
3. **[[surface]]**: the 3D graph z = f(x,y) of a function of two variables.
4. **[[Jacobian]]**: the matrix of all first-order partial derivatives of a vector-valued function.
6. Real models rarely depend on one input. A **multivariable function** takes several inputs and produces one output. **Partial derivatives** measure change in one direction while holding others fixed.
