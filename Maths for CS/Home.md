## Course Folders
- [[01 MOC - Number Foundations]] - Build arithmetic fluency from zero: numbers, operations, fractions, ratios, and checking work.
- [[02 MOC - Algebra]] - Turn arithmetic into general rules using symbols, expressions, equations, and rearrangement.
- [[03 MOC - Functions and Graphs]] - Understand inputs, outputs, mappings, graph shapes, logs, exponentials, and transformations.
- [[04 MOC - Geometry and Trig]] - Learn shape, angle, triangle, circle, vector, and trigonometric reasoning.
- [[05 MOC - Sets Logic and Proof]] - Build the language of rigorous CS reasoning: sets, logic, Boolean rules, and proof.
- [[06 MOC - Discrete Maths]] - Study countable structures used throughout algorithms, data structures, cryptography, and theory.
- [[07 MOC - Linear Algebra]] - Build vector and matrix reasoning for graphics, ML, numerical computing, and systems.
- [[08 MOC - Probability and Statistics]] - Reason under uncertainty, summarise data, test claims, and prepare for ML.
- [[09 MOC - Calculus]] - Study change, accumulation, optimisation, and multivariable gradients.
- [[10 MOC - CS Applications]] - Tie mathematical tools directly to algorithms, systems, security, data, ML, and graphics.
- [[11 MOC - Advanced Topics]] - Prepare for master's-level AI, algorithms, security, theory, and research-heavy CS.
